> [!hint]- Note from G-man
> This website wouldn't have been made 5 years ago, as I've never been technically-proficient (regarding coding especially). I've always tried to innovate the way I play TTRPGs and to that end I've explored ambience apps, VTTs, Discord bots, interactive sheets, cartography, relationship maps. The TTRPG community is vast and full of people that want to make it better and more dynamic. 
> I've always had my focus on the crafts of storytelling, narration and production (no matter how little the budget). With this wiki, as well as its predecessors (the [Miraheze Wiki](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Main_Page) & the [Fandom Wiki](https://gmanwod.fandom.com/wiki/G-MAN%27s_WORLD_OF_DARKNESS_Wiki)), I've had the opportunity to learn how to structure an open-source effort into something understandable both to me and my players.
> 
> I'm not a fan of AI. I don't like the way it's shoved down everyone's throats, I don't like its impact on the world (in any aspect)[^1] or the people behind it. Hell, LLMs are not **AI**, for fucks sake!
> But it has undoubtedly been a useful tool in my journey to build a readable website for me and my players. It's helped me troubleshoot bugs, create **character sheets** that save in-browser and just construct a stylistic wiki that's full of substance - real, human substance.
> 
> If I can direct one tiny fraction of this evil to bring light into a small corner of the globe on the internet, I think that might just be worth trying. > 
> That is, until we pull [a Johnny Silverhand](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Night_City_Holocaust) on them all or, even better, pull [a Rache Bartmoss](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash) on 'em and run our shit locally.

Artificial Intelligence tools like Google Gemini, Claude AI, GitHub Copilot & Cline have been used in the creation and maintenance of the technical backend of this website.
This includes but is not limited to: CSS personalization, custom JavaScript code, HTML pages for interactive character sheets, modifying the Digital Garden plugin.

Artificial Intelligence has not aided in the creation or the modification of any creative content. 
No AI was used when writing/creating any of the user-facing text or images. 

Since this is the case, we do not claim any authorship of the codebase for this website.
