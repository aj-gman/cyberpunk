I go by the same identity on most places online, although I sometimes use the moniker G-man, especially when Storytelling tabletop RPGs.

**I am deeply interested in game design and the philosophy of games & sport, which goes hand in hand with my passion for sociology & politics.** This enables me to create seamless worlds that are vividly life-like. I am a political science graduate and I've been running TTRPGs since 2018, starting out with Cyberpunk 2020, followed by Cyberpunk RED. I've tried my hand at CY_BORG & Mork Borg. I've played and ran ECOPUNK 2044, Nights Black Agents, FIST and other indie titles. Right now, I'm deeply invested in Vampire: The Requiem after a 1 year stint into Vampire: The Masquerade. My obsession is creating and refining mechanics that are specifically useful for specific players.

_I believe that there is no perfect system, only good systems for specific players._ This is also my belief politically, where I align myself with the anarchist movement, doing my part in that. I love DIY, folk punk and I hate landlords.

**I manage my own wiki,** where I endeavor to crystalize my tabletop roleplaying games, [which you can find here](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Main_Page).
