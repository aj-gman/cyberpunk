#news ZIG-POST 158901295-lavn2 #eurotour **April 2021,** [[Eurotour/Adventures/4.5 Hunting the Hunter\|4.5 Hunting the Hunter]]

Five missing persons cases were answered this morning as a blood trail was discovered leading down into a ravine near the Hilton-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Wortrac\|Wortrac]] Lodge in Bern, Switzerland. Of the five, only two are still alive, although in grave condition, at the Intensive Care Unit in [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]]'s Bern Clinic.
![PIC-crackedige.png\|500](/img/user/Pictures/PIC-crackedige.png)

While the victims' identities have been kept a secret for now by the Bern Police Unite, leaks from the institution indicate that at least one of the victims was tied to the [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] tour in [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|Europe]], namely the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]]'s on-and-off input of 5 years, [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]]. 
![PIC-tammi2.jpg\|500](/img/user/Pictures/PIC-tammi2.jpg)
Multiple eye witnesses reported a "wicked party" going on in the nightlife district of Bern. Heading that party was none other than a drunk [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]], constantly grinding on [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]], a detail vividly described to us on multiple accounts. Whether or not [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] is among the five casualties remains to be seen upon his appearance (or lack there of) in [[Eurotour/Adventures/4 Menace in Venice\|Venice]].
![PIC-party.png\|500](/img/user/Pictures/PIC-party.png)
Local rumors correlate with leaks from inside the police department that the ravine where the five bodies were found seems to have been a naturally-occuring [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] deadzone, where coverage was limited, which would explain the slow response of the medical professionals on the scene. Police are currently investigating this coincidence as a possible sign of foul play...
