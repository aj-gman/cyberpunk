#news #eurotour 
[[Eurotour/Adventures/4 Menace in Venice\|Venice]] is up in flames after last night's [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] concert. Despite what many of his fans say, it is a miracle that [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy's]] stage performance - a floating mess of cables, wires, speakers and pyrotechnics - was even approved by the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]], let alone actually survived the waters of [[Eurotour/Adventures/4 Menace in Venice\|Venice]].

But you came here for the juicy deets - did [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] blow up? Well, the short answer is no, although don't get your panties in a twist! Word on the street is that kimono-wearing ninjas (biiiiig loooook towards [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|'saka counterintel]]) took to the canals to eliminate **Alessandro Benetti**, a local trattoria owner. This led us down an interesting path: we questioned Signore **Benneti**, which described what we can only assume were some [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|poorly dressed undercovers from across the pond]]. Eventually, we reached where we found breadcrumbs last time: a bomb plot!

Remember that moment during the concert when [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]] grabbed a chainsaw from backstage and began cutting a portion of the stage? Apparently, `anonymous sources from amongst the workers`  claim that someone had rigged a nuclear apparatus enough to detonate the entire Veneto region!

Although [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jimenez\|Entropy's manager]] has declined to comment, stories on his [[Ziggurat\|Ziggurat]] account keep hinting at the "looming war between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|the Bear]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|the Bull]]" - is this another marketing stunt for the upcoming [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] concert set for next year, engineered by [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Catherine Clements\|Entropy's very own deadly blonde]]?
