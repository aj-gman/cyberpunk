#location #4cw 
Once the capital of the Eastern world, [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]] was hit quite hard during the Soviet years and the [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] has deprioritized it even further, perceiving it as the "crown jewel" of the Tsardom. As such, nearly the entire city has sunk into poverty and an "underworld" market - eurodollars mean nothing, rubles even less. 

People trade in favors, debts, goods and services - I look the other way, you look the other way some other time. Such a system is not friendly to outsiders, "tourists".

...those who are lucky become **EDGERUNNERS**, a new name for enterprising criminals. They work alone or in small groups but refuse to associate with a gang or the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]], relying on third-party [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Fixer\|Fixers]] for jobs. Most **EDGERUNNERS** in [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]] have only seen [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] on the NET, blended with [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]] and anti-corp sentiment. Most **EDGERUNNERS** see the likes of [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] and [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Johnny Silverhand\|Johnny Silverhand]] as incredible inspiration and *actual edgerunners*, so they adopt their values or incorporate them in their own struggles (anti-authoritarian, anarchist, anti-socialist etc.)

### Central (Vasilyevsky Island)
...the sprawling brutalist buildings built to serve the people, surrounded by statues of past Soviet leaders with holographic add-ons, speeches to motivate the workers...

all bridges to and from are guarded by automated defenses and checkpoints, with a squadron of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]]-geared [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] soldiers arriving in d6 minutes from a nearby garrison.

There is no reason you'd wanna be here.

### Industrial
...the walled northern part of "Petrograd", where dreams go to die. Heavy industry, oil tankers, fuel processing and many buses coming in and out. Like [[Eurotour/Locations/Rhine-Ruhr Conurb\|Rhine-Ruhr Conurb]], but dirtier, less secure. 

Very few things here are automated - bus drivers, checkpoint security etc. are all handled by people, older or younger, in order to meet the demand for jobs. 

Everyone has a job in [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]] and they have *just enough* to put on the table. Kibble all the way, unless you know a few guys.

### In-between
Historical buildings are now squatter households or repurposed by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] subsidiaries into watchtowers, living quarters for their soldiers etc.
...except for Mariinsky Theatre, where [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Fixer\|Fixers]] brush shoulders with GRU veterans and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]] heads.

Most soviet apartment blocks are in disrepair, although there's constantly a decades-old [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] robot cleaning up the wiring or putting paint in a corner.

People have been compacted into megabuildings after the collapse of the USSR (post 1990s), the "next logical step" after soviet monoliths. While not ghettos in the traditional sense (of "made-up" housing from metal plates), most residential zoning in [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]] is low-cost, rent-free (not counting the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|"protection" money]]), dangerous and dirty. Most people are sick, can't afford maintenance for their [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware]], indulge in [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Wireheading\|Wireheading]] or [[Braindance\|Braindance]] and rely on kibble or stealing to afford anything.

There is a constant **smog** outside that engulfs everything. You can't quite see 50 meters in front of you and it's hard to breathe even with a mask - and **anti-smog masks** are expensive and even those that you *can get* might be counterfeit, courtesy of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]]'s lesser syndicates.
- Players receive a danger die when shooting, an action die when dodging, and a danger die on prolonged exposure to the outside.
