## Problem 2: the industrial hellscape that is the [Rhine-Ruhr Conurb/Megalopolis](/eurotour/locations/rhine-ruhr-conurb/).
Since The Collapse, Germany's been going through it, hard. In the past 15 years, they've rebuilt almost everything from the ground up, including the restoration of wonderful 19th century architecture in Berlin, Frankfurt, Munchen. The [[Eurotour/Locations/Rhine-Ruhr Conurb\|Rhine-Ruhr Conurb]] was on the backburner of this trend - the German government chose to allow Megacorps to settle in, they did away with taxes on industry. 

The result is **Die Sprawl** - buildings on top of buildings on top of powerplants and recycling units. The Autobahn-Maglev line goes straight through all of the ex-cities (now districts of the Megalopolis), cutting on commute times massively. Most companies also supply shuttle buses from and to the Maglev line, since owning a personal car is *very, very expensive in Germany*.
