#location #eurotour 
The Airport's history is drowned in blood, as it didn't have where to extend about 30 years back. Now, there's an entire city built around the airport, including ghettos. Fortunately, [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Execs]] and the like have access to the Maglev that splits into every direction into the heart of [[Eurotour/Locations/Germany\|Germany]]. Most people, however, have to navigate the underbelly of shady cabs, authorization processes, hotel bookings and whatnot right from the lobby of the [[Eurotour/Locations/Frankfurt Airport\|Frankfurt Airport]]. 
**Plenty of cargo traffic.**
The rumors about the FAG[^1] (Flughafen AG) buying an antimissile system are true...
