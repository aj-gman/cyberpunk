[[Eurotour/Locations/Stockholm\|Stockholm]] is **very cold and very toxic on its outskirts.**

I'll keep it short, gents. It ain't lookin' good for Jack Entropy. The shows in Athens and Budapest were cancelled and I honestly thought I'd find you in the Roman catacombs after that 3-day paid leave. We - sorry, you - are headed to Stockholm, capital of the Scandinavian block. It's colder than a Netrunner's ice bath, so pack heavily. Ray might be able to smuggle your weapons, but you're looking at Cyberware inhibitors. 

Stockholm is nothing too much to write home about - tourist hotspot like Venice (but not that much), riotous Rockerboy scene (but not like Liverpool), full of rich politicos (but not like Bruxelles) and full of sad corpos (but not like Paris). It's heavilly polluted after you reach the city's limits - the countryside is dreary, full of acid rain/snow. Don't forget the NUSSR is only a few clicks away. Permanent night this time of year, so you're expecting less visibility, a lot of fog, but it's not like Sweden's known for its aggresive residents. This isn't the UK. Stay frosty.
