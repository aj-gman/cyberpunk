The [[Eurotour/EYE\|EYE]] is the premier mass surveillance tool made by the [[Eurotour/Locations/United Kingdom\|United Kingdom]] government, the [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|MLA]], in an effort to curb crime. Atop many government buildings, [[Eurotour/EYE\|EYE]] systems use lasers to pierce through just about anything, scanning for crimes.

The EYE system was created in collaboration with Taiwan's [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Kang Tao\|Kang Tao]] in a diplomatic bind that resulted in the influx of Hong Kong refugees that eventually brought the [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|MLA]] to power. Initially an [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Orbital Air\|Orbital Air]] prototype constructed during the [[First Corporate War\|First Corporate War]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Kang Tao\|Kang Tao]]'s upgrades have repurposed it into an information-gathering tool with minimal effort. The process is simple: an AI mind judges everyone's actions (based on their past behaviors) and yields a majority report based on probable consequences. If the then-fugitive is a high-risk target, a [[Eurotour/Organizations/PKY\|PKY]] squad will be deployed to their location, which is constantly tracked by the [[Eurotour/EYE\|EYE]] mechanism.

However, it has a large bypass courtesy of the [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|MLA]]: the Lord's Knights are virtually unscannable, usually due to being granted preferential treatment by one of the Authority's Lords. The Metropolitan Police, [[Eurotour/Organizations/PKY\|PKY]] and foreigners do not share this benefit. 

However, the streets do offer some **Knights' Passes** (6000eb) for exorbitant prices, if street scum can scrounge the money.
