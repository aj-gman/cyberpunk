#gig #eurotour  

## Prepping
**Premise:** [[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] uncovers a bomb plot to kill [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] while he's performing on a floating stage in Venice. The plot is part of a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Wortrac\|Wortrac]] (operating with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/CINO\|CINO]]) gambit to bring down their tourism rivals [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Seal GmbH\|Seal GmbH]] (operating with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/OTEC\|OTEC]]), with whom they've had a low-scale corporate war for about a year.

### Theme
- Can [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] be saved?
	- ...or is he justified in his destruction?
	- ...or is he justified in his depressive anger?
