## Running

### Setup
[[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] arrive in [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]] after joining up the Fight Club aboard the Invisible Pterodactyl, a decommissioned [[Second Corporate War\|Second Corporate War]] military vehicle that is used by the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Nomads of the Lithuanian Free State]] to smuggle weapons and food into the [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]]. 

They aren't immediately tagged as foreigners, as they've picked up some skill-chips from the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Nomads]]. However, this entire trip was quite costly: negotiate among [[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] the **major cost** they had to pay (money/favor) in order to arrive to [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] safely.

- [x] Survive the Pit
- [x] Learn information about the [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] that they did not know already.
- [x] Secure a safehouse in [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]].
	- [x] Fake names, passports and someone who doesn't ask questions. Hotels are out of the question, as most are outfitted with CCTV. Or, perhaps, you don't plan on staying - sleep in the AV?
	- [x] [[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] figured out
