#eurotour 

First #gig in the [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]], Paper Trail takes place in [[Eurotour/Locations/Liverpool\|Liverpool]], [[Eurotour/Locations/United Kingdom\|UK]], as [[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] track down [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] through a city under lockdown. Along the journey they discover a broken [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tinker\|Tinker]] and the more "primal" side of [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]]'s drug-fueled frenzies.

## Prepping
### Theme
- **FISH OUT OF WATER** - Foreigners in a strange place, which operates by similar, yet quite obscurely different rules;
- **DUMPED INTO THE INFESTED SEWAGE** - Corruption on every street corner, dirty people paying dirty money. Dirty airport.
- **OF THE WORLD'S WORST TREATMENT PLANT** - Industry, Pollution, Hate.
