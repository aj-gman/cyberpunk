#organization as part of the [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|MLA]]
*Peacekeepers Ypsilon (PK-Y), the Horsemen, "Pe-Kaii"*

The [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|MLA]]'s premier task force, the PKY might take long to arrive, but their agility, precision and deadliness are unmatched by any other unit in the world. 

+17

Outfitted with [[Rules/CPRED/Combat/Combat#Armor\|Heavy Armorjack]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware#Psychological and Neural\|Sandevistans]], as well as [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware#Physical, generally noticeable\|visible Skeleton implants]] to be able to more efficiently carry all of that armor. Exceptional training and cost means they are dispatched to the lower zones only in the most dangerous of cases, usually in accordance to the [[Eurotour/EYE\|EYE]]'s judgement. For weapons, they carry **[[Rules/CPRED/Combat/Power Guns\|Power Guns]] [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] Assault Rifles** specialized for Full Autofire.

The chosen mode of transportation are chromed-up horses courtesy of [[Biotehnica\|Biotehnica]]'s latest experiments, with very few organics in their body. Their AI is fast and comprehensible, allowing the [[Eurotour/Organizations/PKY\|PKY]] to gain an advantage in any chase, on foot or by vehicle. While not being quite as fast as a car, the infernal traffic of the [[Eurotour/Locations/United Kingdom\|United Kingdom]]'s largest cities assures that the [[Eurotour/Organizations/PKY\|PKY]] still retains the advantage.
