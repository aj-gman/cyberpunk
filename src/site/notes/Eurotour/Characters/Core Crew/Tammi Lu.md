#npc #eurotour 
[[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Fixer\|Fixer]], somehow. Ornament for [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]].
[[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] and [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]] met while both were **studying Law in New York**. They fell for each other hard, but their relationship deteriorated quite quickly, as they struggled to deal with their personal lives ([[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] was becoming a succesful [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]] with a billion fangirls, [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]] had a promising career as an [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]] in a Japanese firm). They eventually resorted to an "open relationship", one where they would have intimate moments with one another, but still play with others to incite jealousy.

This is how [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]] eventually hooked up with [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]], desiring nothing more but [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack's]] attention, too busy with other girls to even notice this. Unfortunately, this context was noticed by [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Catherine Clements\|Catherine Clements]] who, desiring to end [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy's]] career for good, placed a hidden camera during the [[Eurotour/Adventures/3 Trapped Animal\|Paris concert]], revealing [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]]'s carnal relations to [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]].

Then, [[Eurotour/News/Glamorous Deaths in the Swiss Alps\|Glamorous Deaths in the Swiss Alps]]/[[Eurotour/The Swiss Affair\|The Swiss Affair]] occured.

### Characteristics
- **Virtue:** Caring about those that bring her any sort of comfort. Truly loyal.
- **Vice:** Consumerism. Buy the newest cars, brands, designers. She loves gifts. She gets angry when people don't treat her right (read: like a princess).
- **Mask:** Temptress, Cool as a cucumber, In control
- **Dirge:** Intellectual, Loyal, Unprocessed Feelings
- **Desire:** to give [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] something he's never truly felt: love.
- **Can't have Desire:** [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]] is her only source of income. If he suddenly loses his brand, she'll be on the street.
