### Characteristics
- **Virtue:** Respect. Honor-bound.
- **Vice:** Perfectionist, especially when it comes to [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]].
- **Mask:** All-over-the-place, easily-flustered, chaotic.
- **Dirge:** Happy-go-lucky, shy, planned but not calculated.
- **Desire:** To make [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]] a worldwide star.
- **Can't achieve Desire:** He can't control every aspect of Entropy's life, which makes him even more obsessed.
