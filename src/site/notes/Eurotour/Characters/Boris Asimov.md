#npc #4cw 

Worked all his life as a wageslave for [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] in eastern Siberia. Eventually worked his way up when he signed a contract to install military-grade [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware]] in both of his arms (read: borgware). He eventually managed to snake his way into working for [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov#Kalashnikov Concern\|Kalashnikov Concern]], where he earned medals of honor for two successful operations. He was honorably discharged in 2017, but returned in 2021 when multiple [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Executives]] were [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Wireheading\|killed via wiredeath]].
