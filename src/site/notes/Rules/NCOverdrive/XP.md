**BOTCH:** You learn from failure, if you survive! Mark +1 experience point each time a check results in a botch. 
**SURVIVED THE JOB:** You got in and got out in (mostly) one piece. It doesn’t matter whether the job was successful. Mark +1 experience point. If the job was particularly difficult the GM might award bonus experience points.

### Advancement
Characters learn and grow from their experiences. This is reflected through advancement. Clear 5 experience points to do one of the following:
• Write a new trademark (to a maximum of 5)
• Write a new edge for any trademark
