Your [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Drive\|Drive]] is what keeps you going. It's what drags you into the bleeding edge of the city's worst, hoping to come out on top. It's a deep-seated **desire** to see *it* through, whatever *it* is.

Your [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Drive\|Drive]] is always linked to a #ideology. The deeper you go to achieve that ultimate goal, you gain bonuses to your rolls. This entrenchment shows that your character is becoming a fanatic for his ideals - a person at 10/10 points in any [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Drive\|Drive]] is far gone and won't be able to see reason in any other #ideology.

- [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|Insurrectionism]] gives bonuses to violence against specific "enemies (of the people)", anti-authority actions, populist discourse;
- [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealism]] gives bonuses to identifying "bad actors", evaluating merit, righteous action, acting against disloyal peers;
- [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] gives bonuses to gaming the system, self-interest at the cost of others, fighting within the system, apathy or carelessness;
- [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]] gives bonuses to actions outside of the system, recruiting people out of the system and into [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]], identifying systemic infrastructure
