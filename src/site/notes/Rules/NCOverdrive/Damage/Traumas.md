Traumas are tags that represent serious injuries or ongoing physical, emotional or psychological harm.There are as many different traumas as there are ways to be harmed.

While [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Damage/Traumas\|Traumas]] apply [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Dice Mechanics/Dice Rolls\|Danger Dice]] to all checks, they’re also roleplaying cues and should influence how you portray a character. 
==Psycho-emotional [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Damage/Traumas\|Traumas]] also lower [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Humanity\|Humanity]].==

1. **BROKEN/CRUSHED**
2. **WOUNDED/PUNCTURED**
3. **LOST**
4. **BRUISED**
5. **RUINED**
6. **WEAKENED**
