**Rule:** Upon dealing 5 or more damage (after calculating Armor SP), the weapon deals an additional d6. This can grant [[Rules/CPRED/Combat/Critical Injuries\|Critical Injuries]] if there are two 6s.

Power weapons are conventional firearms, in the sense that they use traditional ammo. This category includes a wide variety of weapons including disposable and single-use polymer guns, revolvers, pistols, submachine guns, light- and heavy machine guns, shotguns, and rifles. High rate of fire accompanied by high recoil is a feature of most fully automatic power weapons.
