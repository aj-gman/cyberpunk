##### Armor
Armor works by reducing incoming damage based on **SP (Stopping Power)**.
Attacker rolls damage. Defender subtracts their SP from the damage. 
- If the result is still positive, Defender takes that amount of damage and reduces their SP by 1. 
- Otherwise, the Defender doesn't take any damage and their armor does not ablate.
[[Rules/Homebrew\|Homebrew]]: All SP values and wear penalties have been significantly lowered.

# Melee Weapons
Although firearms are the most popular choice, melee weapons occupy a niche slot, with knives and blades serving to cut through almost anything with monomolecular blades and blunt objects being a good tool to knock anybody unconscious or ruin some [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|tech]]. Below is an archetype list of melee weapons available in [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberpunk RED (HUB)\|Cyberpunk RED (HUB)]].

Melee Weapons cut through armor like butter - when attacking with a melee weapon, the SP value of the defender is reduced by half (rounding down). 

Most weapons are scavenged or repurposed from other fields (sports, industry, kitchen utensils). However, some manufacturers exist specifically for combat-oriented melee weapons. These are:
- [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Kendachi\|Kendachi]]
- [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Budget Arms\|Budget Arms]]
- [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]
