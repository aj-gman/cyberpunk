> [[Rules/CPRED/Quality\|Common Price:]] Everyday (20-50eb)

A type of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drugs\|street drug]] that sees widespread use in Europe, based on the cannabis plant, although its production is limited stateside by [[Biotehnica\|Biotehnica]] copyright and seed property. It is a mild euphoric and psychedellic.

### Main Effect
- **+2 COOL**, [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Psychedelic\|Psychedelic]]
- Strength: 3

### Side Effect
- Test **DR 13 Resist T/D** or suffer from [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Drowsiness\|Drowsiness]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Hunger\|Hunger]]
