> [[Rules/CPRED/Quality\|Common Price:]] Costly (50-100eb)

A type of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drugs\|drug]] that comes in form of black pills used for the clinical treatment of [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|About Cyberpsychosis]][^1]. Prolonged use is associated with Nerve Degeneration, with many pacients ending up bed-ridden or empty-headed.

### Main Effect
- Lowers [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware#Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis Tracker]] by up to -5

### Side Effect
- Check **DR 17 Resist Torture/Drugs**; on a failure, reduce the main effect by 1 (from -5 to -4, from -4 to -3 and so on, up to 0)

### After effects
- If the [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Omega Blockers#Side Effect\|Resist T/D]] is failed, suffer from **[[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Nerve Degeneration\|Nerve Degeneration]]** (+1). Once you reach Nerve Degeneration 5, reset it and lower INT, REF or DEX by -1.

[^1]: See [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware]] for additional details.
