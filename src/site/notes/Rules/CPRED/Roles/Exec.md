#role
Corporate power brokers and business raiders fighting to keep the rule of the Megacorps.

### Role Ability
**Teamwork** - Just like a real corporate executive, the Exec builds a team whose members help them accomplish their goals, whether legal or not, morale permitting. Team members have a visible job description (like secretary or driver) but also have a covert roles (such as Netrunner, bodyguard, or assassin). Plus they get free housing and nice set of clothes!
