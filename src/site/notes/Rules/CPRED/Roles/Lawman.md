#role
The lawmakers of an area, government, corpo, ganggoon, patrolling the mean streets and keeping the peace.

### Role Ability
**Backup** - With this ability, Lawmen can call upon the help of a group of fellow organization members, based on the Lawman's Rank and the conditions under which they make the call. This Backup is armed and dangerous, based on their Rank.
