#role
Rock-and-roll rebels who use performance, art, and rhetoric to fight authority.

## Role Ability
**Charismatic Impact** - a Rockerboy can influence others by sheer presence of personality. They need not be a musical performer; they can influence others through poetry, art, dance, or simply their physical presence. They could be a rocker—or a cult leader.
