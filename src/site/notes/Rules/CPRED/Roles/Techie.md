#role
Renegade mechanics and supertech inventors; the people who make the Dark Future run.

### Role Ability
**Maker** - Using the Maker Role Ability, the Tech can fix, improve, modify, make, and invent new items. Whenever a Tech increases their Maker Rank by one, they gain one rank in two different Maker Specialties of their choice, including **repairing, upgrading, fabricating, and inventing**
