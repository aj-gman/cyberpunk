#role
Reporters, media stars, and social influencers risking it all for the truth—or glory.

### Role Ability
**Credibility** - The Media can not only convince an audience of the truth of what they publish but also has a larger audience the more credible they are. Medias also have greater levels of access to sources and information; they are always in the know with their ears to the ground to pick up on rumors and information passively
