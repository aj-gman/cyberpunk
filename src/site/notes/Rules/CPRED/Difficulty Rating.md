- **9, Simple** - Most people can do without thinking.
- **13, Everyday** – Most people can accomplish without specialized training.    
- **15, Difficult** – Notable effort and is hard to achieve without training or natural aptitude.
- **17, Professional** – Formal training, marking the individual as a skilled professional.
- **21, Heroic** – High-skill achievement attainable only by top performers and elite specialists.
- **24, Incredible** – Exceptional feat, placing the achiever among the best in their field.
- **29, Legendary** – Rare, awe-inspiring accomplishment remembered and retold for years.
