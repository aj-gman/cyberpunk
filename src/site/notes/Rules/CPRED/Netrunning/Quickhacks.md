Quickhacks require an [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware Classes#Psychological and Neural\|Operating System]] in order to function. They can only target one person at a time and require the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] to mentally overpower their target before being initiated.

In order to fully upload a Quickhack, a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] must go through the process: 
**JACK IN > BREACH > QUICKHACK > JACK OUT**

#### JACK IN (NEUROPORT)
[[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] rolls d10+INTERFACE against their target's d10+WILL. 
- On a fail, the target knows they're being hacked. If so, they can then attempt a Concentration check against the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]]'s INTERFACE to kick them out of their system.

#### BREACH
Some savvy people install Self-ICE in their Neuroport, preventing a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] from quickly deploying a program. In this case, Self-ICE stalls the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]], prompting successive checks (1 x DR6 or 2x DR8 or 3x DR10 etc.).
- On a fail, the target knowns they're being hacked (if they didn't already). Furthermore, some Self-ICE can disconnect the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] or inflict damage to their brain.

####  DEPLOY
To deploy a Quickhack, the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] rolls d10+INTERFACE against the Quickhack's DV. 
- On a fail, the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] is unsafely Jacked Out, taking damage.
