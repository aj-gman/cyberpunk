#boostergang [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]]
By [2020](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2020 "2020"), the Blood Razors in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] were on top of the gang chain in the local combat zone, being considered one of the largest groups participating there. It was rumored that something called **RazorBall** was played in an abandoned ice-skating rink in [[Night City/Others/Combat Zone\|the Zone]] once every month, which had a great turnout [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Boostergangs of Night City\|among the city gangs]].
