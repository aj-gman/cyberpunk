>[!warning]- In 2077
A gang that controls the districts of [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Watson\|Watson]] and the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Northside\|Northside]] Industrial District (NID). You can easily recognize them by their intimidating faceplates and hostile optic implants, which not only indicates their gang affiliation but also their fetishization of [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|cyberware]] and cybermodification. 
During the [[Night City/Gigs/2077/Electric Tornado\|"Electric Tornado" Crisis]], [[Night City/Characters/2077/Cross\|Cross]], [[Night City/Characters/2077/Warlock\|Warlock]] and [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]], three Maelstrom higher-ups, were spotted negotiating with an up-and-coming Media from Pacifica, [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]]. 

[^1]: There is a singular leader that controls the philosophy and the goals of the gang, with different subordinates being put in place to assure the good functioning of the chosen activities. Any of the leaders can be (and have been) dethroned by "might makes right" ideology, through duels, murders or street fights.

[^2]: Unverified claim supported only by independent [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]] investigations.
