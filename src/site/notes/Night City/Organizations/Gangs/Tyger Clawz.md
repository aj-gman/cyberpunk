w#boostergang [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]
![LOGO-TYGERCLAWZ.png\|228](/img/user/Pictures/LOGO-TYGERCLAWZ.png)

In preparation for [[Night City/Fourth Corporate War\|upcoming war]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] realized that they'd need to have the people of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] by their side if they were going to win. They weren't going up against the Mob anymore. So they formed a guardian gang, initially a Motorcycle Club, that evolved into a Crime Syndicate to protect [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Asia\|Little Asia]] from attacks.
