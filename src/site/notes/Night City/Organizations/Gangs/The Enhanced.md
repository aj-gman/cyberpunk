**Noun:** Enhanced
**Symbols:** Brains, Wiring, Short-circuits, "Zapping"
>**Structure:** Accelerationist Doomsday Cult[^1]
>**Activities:** [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Damage/Weapon Tags\|Gun trafficking]], Black-hat [[Rules/CPRED/Netrunning/Netrunning\|Netrunning]], terrorism, [nihilistic violent extremism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nihilistic_violent_extremism), [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Wireheading\|facilitation of Wireheading]], [[Braindance\|BD production and distribution]]

>[!warning] Areas of operation
>- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center\|Corporate Center]], across various corporate headquarters, as well as parks or shops;
>- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Westside\|Old Downtown]], especially in less-visited skyscrapers

>[!tip] Known Members
>- [[Blue Land\|Blue Land]], possible leader of the organization

In the 2020s, [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Enhanced\|The Enhanced]] formed out of the remains of [the Brainiacs](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Brainiacs), who were systematically massacred by the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|Blood Razors]]. This animosity had been sparked when [the Brainiacs](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Brainiacs) made a chapter of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|Blood Razors]] immediately collapse due to an unforeseen [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] raid.

[[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Enhanced\|The Enhanced]], understanding their weakness in open combat, adopted a secretive stance compared to their precursors. They became a secretive cult that used their members' skills to enact dangerous activities to get more of the public [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Cyberware\|chipped in]].
