#organization
**MaxTac (Maximum Force Tactical Division)** is a specialized sub-group of the [[Night City/Organizations/NCPD\|NCPD]] tactical ops division. As a [C-SWAT](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/C-SWAT "C-SWAT"), [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MaxTac]] specializes in dealing with dangerous [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis Symptoms\|cyberpsychos]]. It has been known by different names such as **Cyborg Suppression Unit**, the **NCPD Psycho Squad**, **Max-Tac** or **MAX-TAC**.
