#corporation 
International Media Technologies

Subsidiary of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]
## Departments
1. **Center on Media Policy**
a think tank where several teams staffed by prominent media researchers and analysts devote their time to predicting what technological trends and developments are worth pursuing.
2. **Technology Research**
the tech department provides the relevant tools for the Center's lofty goals, as well as keeping [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] at the bleeding edge of [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]] tech, from [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Cyberware\|subdermal cheek optics]] to [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Gear\|self-destructing trackers]].
3. **Special Operations**
industrial espionage and asset extraction[^1], as well as many other undocumented SpecOps

[^1]: included but not limited to [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techies]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Execs]] from other corps, media recovery, data forgery
