#organization #eurotour 

**CINO** **(Corporation Internationale Nauticale et Oceanique)** was a French oceanographic and shipbuilding operations corporation, which specialized in underwater shipping and technology services around the [Pacific Rim](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Pacific_Rim "Pacific Rim") and Atlantic Rim. When the German corporation [IHAG](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/IHAG "IHAG") declared bankruptcy, CINO became embroiled in a conflict with rival corporation [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/OTEC\|OTEC]] over IHAG's remaining assets. As CINO hired the [Arasaka](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Arasaka "Arasaka") to fight on their behalf against OTEC's proxy, [Militech](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Militech "Militech"), the conflict escalated and led to the [Fourth Corporate War](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Corporate_War "Fourth Corporate War").

CINO's influence in [[Eurotour/Adventures/4 Menace in Venice\|Italy]] is determined by their subsidiary, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Wortrac\|Wortrac]], a powerful corporate actor in the parliament of Italy and also the determinant tourist factor in most of Italy.
