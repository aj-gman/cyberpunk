#corporation 

>[!note] This page was written by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Emi]], the player behind [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]].

#### TICKER SYMBOL: N54
![Logo-N54.png\|267](/img/user/Pictures/Logo-N54.png)

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] owns, controls or manages approximately a third of the world’s media industry, from films to news to porn to music to [[Braindance\|braindances]]. They employ [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Medias]] that are willing to risk everything to get a good story and focus more on the *"boots-on-the-ground"* than [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] would be likely to believe. 

Their reach and near-monopoly on broadcasted information continues to attract people from all walks of life willing to get into the fold. [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] is willing to give them a platform as long as its profitable. And most of the time, revealing a juicy story about some [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]] gets way more clicks than a canceled [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] sponsorship or hush money.

## Origins
[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|CableNews 54]] was founded by Erich R. Dreyer in [[Night City/Organizations/States/NUSA\|the USA]] ==in 1991== as a cable news service, developing a reputation for tough, accurate, hard-hitting journalism while reporting on [the First Central American War](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/First_Central_American_War), [[CHOOH2\|CHOOH2]]’s development and other events at the time.

Giving [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN) rough competition, CN54’s quality earned it the success which allowed it to diversify and expand, gaining control of several smaller cable networks.

CN54 bought [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) ==in 1998==, expanding from cable to the broadcast airwaves and renaming itself Network News 54. N54 diversified its programming into all varieties of entertainment, becoming the first mediacorp to exert major control over both cable and broadcast. Still, the investigative journalist spirit did not cease, as [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] published articles on the assassination of [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Richard Night\|Richard Night]] and the formation of the [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|Martial Law Authority]] in the [[Eurotour/Locations/United Kingdom\|UK]].

## Apex
Having achieved the status of a media tycoon, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] created [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/IMT\|IMT]], a subsidiary dedicated entirely to R&D, and financed well enough that it could compete with dedicated tech corps. [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/IMT\|IMT]] is perhaps [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]'s most fascinating project, as it allows it to break the mold and provide [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]]-like services to specific clients.

The efforts of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/IMT\|IMT]] lead to [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] also becoming the first mediacorp with a strong in-house R&D capability, while other mediacorps were still buying tech from other competitors like Sony (bought by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] in 2020), Microtech (the premier vendor of computers) or Yamaha (known for its quality musical instruments among [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]] channels).

Although the other mediacorps caught on eventually, ==by 2001== [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]’s head start established it as the dominant corp in mediatech development, having been on the cutting edge ever since.

==In 2002==, N54 popularized the online news service: the news of the Food Crash, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]]’s declaration of privatization and California’s secession were read by the public through N54’s online screamsheets.

==In 2005==, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] introduced **Soundchips** –widely available small ROM chips, containing up to 3 songs, which, when plugged in, send out a signal which allows the wearer to experience music in flawless, 360 degree stereo brilliance. Easier and cheaper to mass produce than digital discs, the Soundchip pushed the underground [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboys]] out, and pulled the sponsored bands in. Llamaboys, the originators of technoise, debuted their `Overlay` album through soundchip, but the tech was truly popularized by [[Samurai\|Samurai]] and their `Chippin’ In` album.

With 25% of the total broadcasting power of the USA in its pocket, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] had contracts for all of the hottest stars and shows and and the best news department in professional media ==in 2005==.

At some point during that year, Howard Wong was headhunted by [[DMS\|Digital Media Systems]], being given the position of CEO. After a number of shrewd business moves which made [[DMS\|DMS]] a true rival to [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], a furious and frequently destructive race between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] and [[DMS\|DMS]] for consolidation of the few remaining independent stations and communications industries followed.

==In 2009==, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] attempted to kidnap Wong, which prompted [[DMS\|DMS]] to assassinate him. A series of retribution extractions and assassinations on both sides followed, which nearly exploded into a full blown corporate war.

## Recent Events
The rivalry between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] and [[DMS\|DMS]] has shifted from the battlefield to the boardroom. 

The two attempt to this day to outdo the other in the development of new technologies, series, stars and in the breaking of major news stories. The competition for acquisition of smaller companies continues unabated. 

N54 also owns several music stations across the world, being a leader in commercial radio.
