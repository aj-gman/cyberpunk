#organization 
Initially a development firm known as Night International, it was later restructured into **Night Foundation** by the late [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Richard Night\|Richard Night]]'s wife, [[Miriam Night\|Miriam Night]]. By [[2077\|2077]], it was reorganized as a corporation, Night Corp.

#### TICKER SYMBOL: NIGHT


## Departments

### NEMA
Night Emergency Management Agency

### NHS
Night Health Service
Manages and operates the various [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]-owned hospital in the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center#Medical Center\|City Center#Medical Center]], like the [Crisis Medical Center](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Crisis_Medical_Center_(2020)) and the [City Medical Center](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/City_Medical_Center).
The [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp#NHS\|NHS]] charges for each stay, with exorbitant sums for any sort of real luxury.
