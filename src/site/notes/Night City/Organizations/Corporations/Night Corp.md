#organization 
Initially a development firm known as Night International, it was later restructured into **Night Foundation** by the late [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Richard Night\|Richard Night]]'s wife, [[Miriam Night\|Miriam Night]]. By [[2077\|2077]], it was reorganized as a corporation, Night Corp.
