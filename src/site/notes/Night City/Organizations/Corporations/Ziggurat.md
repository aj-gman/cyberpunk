#corporation 
Subsidiary of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/IEC\|IEC]] focused on social media, especially their one-stop-shop platform, **Garden**, where users can play games, interact with one another in *relative* secrecy, publish posts, comment publicly and so on.

While other means of communicating digitally have appeared, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Ziggurat\|Ziggurat]] has been quick to overshadow any real competitors.
