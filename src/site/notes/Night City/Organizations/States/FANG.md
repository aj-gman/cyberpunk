#organization 
**Federal Anti-Nemesis Guard**

Created in collaboration with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] after the [Gang of Four](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Gang_of_Four)'s crashing of the federal democracy in order to weed out those accused of treason.
The [[Night City/Organizations/States/NUSA\|NUSA]] acquired the license to produce [[Night City/Others/Technology/IEC Dragoon\|Dragoons]] themselves and signed a contract (bypassing auctions) with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] to both build the [[Fullborg\|Fullborg]] and train its user.

By 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/States/FANG\|FANG]] had approximatively █████████ operating.
