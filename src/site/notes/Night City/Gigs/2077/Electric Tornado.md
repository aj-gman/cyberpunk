#gig
A [[Templates/Gig\|Gig]] set in [[2077\|2077]], where the players, acting as first responders from [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]], must deal with internal strife (a rogue mildly-[[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|cyberpsycho]] agent) as well as an active hostage situation(?). [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] is threatening to bring an entire powerplant to the ground, all the while two other squads ([[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] troops and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]]) are also landing with their own objectives.

## Prepping
#### Theme
Teamwork makes the dream work. But does it when you can't choose your mates?
#### Mood
- Daylight, working hours, **everybody except those inside** are having the time of their lives (or so it seems)
- Dark hallways and server rooms, heavy industrial machinery; purposefully closed off
- Dehumanization through [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware]] and heavy [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|About Cyberpsychosis]]
#### Motifs
- internal strife when [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|an agent focused on killing]] makes the entire mission more dangerous by just being there
- conflict generated by competition between corporations, with infighting between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] and [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]].
- external conflict between a risky hostage situation with [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|chromed-up criminals]] and their noble goals - in fact, they did publish their demands publicly and they have [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|a Media working with them]], appearing as one of the "hostages"


## Running
### Setup
- [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] is a dangerous boostergang with hideouts all across [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Northside\|Northside]], which is where the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Locations/Microwave Powerplant\|Microwave Powerplant]] is located. 
	- They have not made any demands, it's been ~2 minutes since the silent alarm was triggered
- Owned by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]], it's very much expected that they've also mobilized security (both inside the building and external) to infiltrate, so it's a race against the time. Expect [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] extracting VIPs as well.
- **Arrest, not terminate.**
- **Entry points:**
	- Sewer system that connects the water canals.
	- Main gate.
	- AV on top of one of the Cooling Blocks.
	- Dropped in the water near the Radar Control Tower.
- Possible **Assets**:
	- [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]] AV circling around the perimeter, with your pilot;
	- Two [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]] officers, [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]] and [[Night City/Characters/2077/Alvarez\|Alvarez]]
	- Blackout
	- Lockdown (triggered by overloading the system)
![[powerplant-map\|1000]]


### Execution
- Every 5 minutes, the terrorists get nervous and do something. Depending on how [[Rules/CBR+PNK/Threat Level\|Threatened]] they feel, the [[Rules/CBR+PNK/Effect Level\|Effect]] of their action will be more dire (directly proportional).
- [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] troops only care about the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Locations/Microwave Powerplant\|Plant's]] safety, so their priority is shutting down all systems by entering the Petrochem offices. They're using explosives, breaching doors and clearing sights with little regard to human lifeforms.
- [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] does not care about anything except their VIPs, which are located in the Old Petrochem buildings, taken hostage a lone wolf cell 
##### The Terrorists
- are split in 3 main groups, each with a leader
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Cross\|Cross]], the most likely to go [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|cyberpsycho]], is locked away in the **Petrochem offices** with d6 [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstromites]], turrets, drones and a bomb. 
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Warlock\|Warlock]] is with the hostages (d6 + [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]]) along with 2 other [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstromites]], but he is keeping stealthy; in the **Old Petrochem Buildings**
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]] is in the Radar Control Tower, stressed and on edge, surrounded by EMPs to try and shut down or blow up the plant; he has 2d6 [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstromites]] patrolling the entire area and a stolen AV
### Turning Point
- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]] loses it, killing civvies and whoever else gets in his way.
- Either [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] or [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] finish executing their maneuver (depending on how the PCs have taken their actions) and secure one of the sites.
- As a last ditch effort, depending on who is left alive:
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Cross\|Cross]] detonates the offices and tries to escape to the sewer. If followed, he goes into CY-RAGE.
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Warlock\|Warlock]] kills 2 hostages and plants remote-detonated explosives inside their guts. He then takes point and uses a sniper to clear a way on the ground.
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]] causes the Plant to go into shutdown, triggering a blackout across [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Watson\|Watson]] and possibly even more.

### End
- Secure [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]], dead or alive.
- Kill or arrest all [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]]. You can leave once you complete this objective. You can also sabotage [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] for the following reason:
	- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]] tells you that the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] are actually doing this because [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] is threatening to cut all electricity to [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Northside\|Northside]], pretty much declaring it a [[Night City/Others/Combat Zone\|Combat Zone]] indirectly;

## After Action Report
This session took place on **04.11.2025**. You can view the [Discord thread here](https://discord.com/channels/1396876455203569817/1396912046108573826/1435313499671892040). The players were Emy & Sasha.
#### Events
- The players, [[Night City/Characters/2077/Joe Barbarossa\|Joe Barbarossa]] & [[Chronodemos\|Chronodemos]], took the role of [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]] officers and quite efficiently penetrated the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Locations/Microwave Powerplant\|Microwave Powerplant]], getting rid of two out of the three [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] lieutenants, namely [[Night City/Characters/2077/Cross\|Cross]] and [[Night City/Characters/2077/Warlock\|Warlock]]. They also cooperated with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] troops briefly, which netted [[Night City/Characters/2077/Joe Barbarossa\|Joe Barbarossa]] with a CRED from them.
- They managed to shut down the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Locations/Microwave Powerplant\|Microwave Powerplant]] together with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and took minimum casualties (besides one of their own dying quite early on, namely [[Night City/Characters/2077/Alvarez\|Alvarez]]).
- They also incapacitated [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]], who they found out was a [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Black Lace\|Black Lace]] user and was experiencing [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|About Cyberpsychosis]] symptoms.
- Later on, they found out a possible connection between [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] and [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]]
- They sustained minimum damage and maintained close connection with the Central Office of [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]], showing **good team etiquette**;

#### Epilogue
- The players found out that [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]] either orchestrated the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Locations/Microwave Powerplant\|Microwave Powerplant]] takeover, with [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] as hired muscle, or vice-versa;
	- An interesting avenue they suggested is that [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]] forcefully withheld the information that the Terrorists had publicly sent out their demands from the [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|MAXTAC]] unit, turning them into a kill squad;
	- In truth, [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]]'s [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]] managed to sneak into some of the systems, installing a [[Night City/Others/Events/Bug's Backdoor\|backdoor]] for later usage. Who aided who remains to be seen as [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]] becomes a larger threat in future sessions.
- The players' actions led to [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]] being forcefully retired at the age of 33, quite early for a member of the [[Night City/Organizations/MAXTAC\|psycho squad]], where investment over time translates into loyalty to the organization even post-mortem.
	- As such, [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]] is now on a shitty public pension that barely lets him afford rent in [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Watson\|Watson]] and not enough money to maintain his military-grade [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|cybernetics]]
	- Still addicted to [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Black Lace\|Black Lace]], it is unsure how he is going to deal with his problems and if he will end up dead or, worse, a [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|cyberpsycho]] let loose.

#### Session Feedback
- The session felt very CYBERPUNK and the [[Night City/Gigs/2077/Electric Tornado#Theme\|#Theme]] was very well regarded and understood, save for a few mishaps that can be corrected.
	- Trauma Team's efficiency could've been  better highlighted through a call from Central telling the players "20 hostages got rescued", coupled with maybe 2-3 AVs instead of just one;
	- Militech's regimented thinking (and poor planning) could've been shown better;
- [[Night City/Characters/2077/Lieutenant Hook\|Lieutenant Hook]] was generally a good character, as the players enjoyed his slow and unsure descent into madness, with the adrenaline slowly being hyped up by the end of the session;
- From my perspective, the music and map were alright, they did their job and were good enough to not notice them too much either way;
- The [[Night City/Gigs/2077/Electric Tornado#Epilogue\|#Epilogue]] was very well executed, with a slow lapse back into reality from in-character discussion to in-character macro-actions to eventually discussing broader feedback, themes and motifs
- Setting the game during midday rush hour was a nice touch in my opinion
