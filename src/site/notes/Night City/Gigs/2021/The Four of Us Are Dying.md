## Premise
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] focus their attention on their next target, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]].

The [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] raids their [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/ASG Headquarters (Heywood)\|ASG Headquarters (Heywood)]] with an arrest warrant for [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Deadmeat\|Deadmeat]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] gets some time to prepare via [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lowkey\|Lowkey]] announcing them. Unfortunately, [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD#NETSEC\|NCPD#NETSEC]] has trapped [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Deadmeat\|Deadmeat]] in the [[Night City/Others/Technology/NET\|NET]], so she can't communicate.
[[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD#Investigation\|NCPD#Investigation]]'s Detective on the scene is [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Coalman\|Coalman]].

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]] can be accessed either via the murky underground of [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Billy Night\|Billy Night]] or through the highrises of [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Mbole Ebunike\|Mbole Ebunike]]. One is paranoid, one is aggressive.

- An in with the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Wild Things\|Wild Things]]: [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lara Maillard\|Lara Maillard]], who comes a-crawling to [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]].
- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center#Civic Center\|To City Hall!]] - [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Santiago Aldecado\|Santiago]] in a suit, court hearing for the [[Night City/Locations/Laguna Bend\|Laguna Bend]] massacre, calling [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] as witnesses; his lawyer, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Thompson\|Thompson]].

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] reinstates [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] as an active member and demands he present himself for a medical checkup in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Hex (GM)\|Ella Michaels]] contacts [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] again, telling him that she was [[Night City/Others/Technology/Soulkiller\|Soulkilled]] and left by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] to rot in the [[Night City/Others/Technology/NET\|NET]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Nat]] also hears this and wants to believe it.
