[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] move to [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Heywood\|Heywood]] (presumably) and begin preparing for the big showdown with [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Iwasao Koremichi\|Iwasao Koremichi]]. Unlucky for them, a lot of shit is catching up with them, and not in the fun way.

## After Action Report
The gang moved to a hybrid three-floor building in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Heywood\|Heywood]], where they used their contacts within [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]] to get an [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|architect]] to do a review of the building's condition. They moved [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Basebuilding\|their gear]] into the new base. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] tried reaching [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Santiago Aldecado\|Santiago Aldecado]], but he said he was "busy raiding corporate convoys".

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] got **a new Linear Frame**, but he had to lean on [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Nat]] in order to get it. This impacted their relationship, since [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] is getting more transactional with it.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] went to the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] in order to recruit a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] for their [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Basebuilding\|base]] and hired [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Deadmeat\|Deadmeat]] as their permanent [[Rules/CPRED/Netrunning/Netrunning\|deep diver]], with [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lowkey\|Lowkey]] as a one-time [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]] for the "gig of a lifetime" they were trying to pull.
They helped [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Deadmeat\|Deadmeat]] move her gear to the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/HQ (Heywood)\|HQ (Heywood)]] and convinced [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Corpse Reviver\|Corpse Reviver]] to help them by sending [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Big Shark\|Big Shark]] to spike a drink.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] planned out how to take out [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Iwasao Koremichi\|Iwasao Koremichi]] (as ordained to them by [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Morgan Blackhand\|Morgan Blackhand]] during [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Seven Nation Army\|Seven Nation Army]]). They did scouting on [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] response times and eventually geared up their own [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] to match perfectly with the system. They followed the guy till he reached the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/Midnight Slut\|Midnight Slut]], a [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] bar.

Unfortunately, they didn't account for one thing: [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Adam Smasher\|the grim reaper coming to collect]]. They pretended to be sent by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] as well, but [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] took off his mask. At that moment, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Adam Smasher\|Smasher]] recognized him. He could've killed him, but instead hounded him by saying *"[[Eurotour/Characters/Claire Monet\|Claire]] gives her regards"* and giving him a concussion. [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Iwasao Koremichi\|Iwasao Koremichi]] had been wiped by [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Adam Smasher\|Adam Smasher]], so [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] resolved to take his corpse, confirm his death, get their pay and then dump him in the [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Badlands\|Badlands]]' trash mountains.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] called [[Eurotour/Characters/Claire Monet\|Claire Monet]], asking her what the fuck was going on. She gave an unsatisfying answer to him - yet again they are on opposite sides, he's playing second fiddle for [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and she's sold her soul (or so it appears) to [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]].

A [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] called [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]], teasing him about catching [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Nat]] and her entire squad.
