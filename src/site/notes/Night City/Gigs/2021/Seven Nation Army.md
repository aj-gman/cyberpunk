## After-Action Report
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]], along with a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Solo\|Solo]] looking for fortune, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Liam Brightburn\|Liam Brightburn]], attended the funeral of [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Ella Michaels]], held at the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/NC Columbarium\|NC Columbarium]] in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Westside\|Westside]]. An unfortunate and unknown accident, spurred on by [[Night City/Others/Technology/weird device\|a weird device]] later discovered by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]], occured. Most of the guards, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]], [[Night City/Organizations/NUSA\|NUSA]] became frenzied, assumingly [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis]], shooting into the crowd.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] managed to defend against the onslaught, although many notable [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]] and [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] were killed in the crossfire. [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] also attended the meeting, stealing [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]]'s gun and shooting into [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Steiger\|Steiger]], putting him dow.

[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] was arrested by the [[Night City/Organizations/NCPD\|NCPD]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] arrived on the scene and [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] barely survived. The entire incident did not appear on [[Night City/Others/Corporate News\|Corporate News]], althought some [[Night City/Others/Independent News\|Independent News]] did cover it.

![AAR-sevennation.png](/img/user/Pictures/AAR-sevennation.png)

[[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|Blood Razors]], [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Red Chrome Legion\|Red Chrome Legion]] are in an alliance. To that end, they've taken to invading [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Asia\|Little Asia]].

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] received a detailed internal report from [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Nat]] that puts the blame heavily on [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Iwasao Koremichi\|Iwasao Koremichi]] for engineering the [[Night City/Others/Events/Militech Executives Die in Poison Attack\|Ella's death]]. [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Morgan Blackhand\|Morgan Blackhand]] came by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]]'s HQ in order to propose an offer - kill [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Iwasao Koremichi\|the District Manager]]. He gave you a time and a place, with more details on the way.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] are planning to use their [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] suits. But first, they need to get a feel for the response time.
