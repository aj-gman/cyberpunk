## After-Action Report
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]], along with a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Solo\|Solo]] looking for fortune, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Liam Brightburn\|Liam Brightburn]], attended the funeral of [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Ella Michaels]], held at the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/NC Columbarium\|NC Columbarium]] in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Westside\|Westside]]. An unfortunate and unknown accident, spurred on by [[weird device\|a weird device]] later discovered by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]], occured. Most of the guards, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]], [[Night City/Organizations/NUSA\|NUSA]] became frenzied, assumingly [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis]], shooting into the crowd.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] managed to defend against the onslaught, although many notable [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]] and [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] were killed in the crossfire. [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] also attended the meeting, stealing [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]]'s gun and shooting into [[Steiger\|Steiger]], putting him dow.

[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] was arrested by the [[Night City/Organizations/NCPD\|NCPD]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] arrived on the scene and [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] barely survived.
