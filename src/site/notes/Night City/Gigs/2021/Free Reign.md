## After Action Report
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] called up multiple contacts to help with the cleaning of their warehouse. 
[[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Nunzio Scallioné\|Nunzio Scallioné]] brought in some boys from [[Halsey, Ferris & Skiv\|Halsey, Ferris & Skiv]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] called [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Laura Aldecado\|Laura Aldecado]] and convinced some [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Aldecaldos\|Aldecaldos]] to come by for some [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Basebuilding\|Basebuilding]]. In-between these two, two [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] vehicles pulled up and tried to take [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] out (unsuccesfully). [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] made a broadcast on [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Ziggurat\|Ziggurat]] threatening the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]]. [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Skell\|Skell]] rose up and challenged [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]].

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] uncovered word of mouth regarding the fact that [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] may be building a [Punknaught](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Punknaught_(2020)), talking to some [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techie]] from [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]]'s past, one [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Savvy\|Savvy]].
They have now arrived at the northern edge of [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]] and they plan to head in with [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Who-Who\|Who-Who]], nicknamed "the Japantown Punisher" by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]].

>[!tip]- Navigating [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]]
>![SS-freereign1.png\|433](/img/user/Pictures/SS-freereign1.png)

Upon descending into [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]], they encountered some [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Solo\|Solo squad]] who wanted to rob them. Their quick thinking eventually ended the situation without blood being spilled. They tried convincing [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Corpse Reviver\|Corpse Reviver]] to join the struggle against the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]], but she backed off.

They climbed on top of a nearby building in order to survey the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] garage. They saw an [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] being taken down and then towed to the building, so they formed a plan to infiltrate it. They disguised as [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstromites]] and got to take a look at [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|the Punknaught]], but there were ~20 [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstromites]] so they backed off. 

>[!tip]- [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze's]] picture of [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|the Punknaught]]
![PIC-THETANK.png\|452](/img/user/Pictures/PIC-THETANK.png)

Eventually, [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] was identified by the gang's leader, [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Easy\|Easy]], due to his in-depth knowledge of vehicle manufacturing. Her past enabled her to smell that something was off. They had a conversation in which [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] agreed to back off. Right as they were leaving, [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Easy\|Easy]] initiated combat.
