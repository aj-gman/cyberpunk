## Premise
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] begins investigating ways to gain influence for their new organization, [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]]. They look for support with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]] (perchance through [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Billy Night\|Billy Night]]?) and with [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]].

[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lowkey\|Lowkey]] returns, explaining his disappearance. He shares what pieces he could retrieve from [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Adam Smasher\|Adam Smasher]]'s indexes about [[Night City/Others/Technology/Soulkiller\|Soulkiller]]. [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]] is quite large now, which means [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] have a large pool of [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] to pick from, lest the [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|new laws]] get in the way.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] must contend with their new-found power and face offers/threats from the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]].

## After-Action Report

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] started with [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]], meeting with [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Theresa Valentino\|Theresa Valentino]] in a public parking lot in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Heywood\|Heywood]]. [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Theresa Valentino\|Theresa Valentino]] noticed [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] studying her. A [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]] took photos of the entire encounter for posterity. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] prospectively agreed to ship and distribute the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|Cartel's]] product in exchange for the ins with some fixers.

Then, [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] met [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Stanislavski\|Stanislavski]] in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Europe#East Marina\|Little Europe#East Marina]], where the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]] proposed that [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]] intervene on behalf of [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]], kicking out [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|the Mob]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] refused and almost had a standoff with [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Stanislavski\|Stanislavski]] in his own den.

Going up to [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/NorthOak\|NorthOak]], they met [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] in prison, where they discussed future plans and assured he'd commit to support them going public with one condition: they get rid of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]]'s strongmen from [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Europe#Little Italy\|Little Europe#Little Italy]] and eliminate their influence from [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Westside\|Westside]].
- 4 borgs are [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] lieutenants or detectives 
- 4 borgs are [[Edgerunners \|Edgerunners ]]
- 4 borgs are [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Fixer\|Fixers]]

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] went after [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]], finding him in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Westbrook\|Westbrook]] of all places, planning to do a terrorist act with a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] pilot, one [[Valerie Wildner\|Valerie Wildner]]. They convinced him to join up with them. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] witnessed [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jackie]] as a sober person for once. They soon managed to organize a block party in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Westbrook\|Westbrook]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray]] instilled some [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelist]] [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Drive\|sentiment]] among the [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]], while [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]] made contact with [FACS](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Far_Asian_Co-Prosperity_Sphere). 

Their last stop on the *tour de force* was [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Santiago Aldecado\|Santiago Aldecado]], which they helped fend off a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|aerial]] attack. He immediately agreed to back them up. They witnessed a romantic moment between [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Santiago Aldecado\|Santiago]] and [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Rogue Amendiares\|Rogue]].
