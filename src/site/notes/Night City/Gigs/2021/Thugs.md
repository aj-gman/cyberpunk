## Premise
[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] begins investigating ways to gain influence for their new organization, [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]]. They look for support with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]] (perchance through [[Billy Night\|Billy Night]]?) and with [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]].

[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lowkey\|Lowkey]] returns, explaining his disappearance. He shares what pieces he could retrieve from [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Adam Smasher\|Adam Smasher]]'s indexes about [[Night City/Others/Technology/Soulkiller\|Soulkiller]]. [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]] is quite large now, which means [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] have a large pool of [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] to pick from, lest the [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|new laws]] get in the way.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] must contend with their new-found power and face offers/threats from the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]].
