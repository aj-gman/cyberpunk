## After Action Report
[[Night City/Gigs/2021/Into the Lion's Den\|Into the Lion's Den]]: 17 [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] [[Night City/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]] were assassinated via poisoning, including [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]]'s mother.

Before all of this: a meeting between three [[Night City/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]]: an [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]], a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]]. Nothing came of it and, worse, the [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]] died mysteriously.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] met with [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]], who treated them like people. He agreed to help, reformatting the deed to the warehouse in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Asia\|Japantown]]

Who ordered [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|The Great Work]]? [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Kei Arasaka\|Kei Arasaka]] himself, through a series of agents
	This information was found out by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] and sent to [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Natalie Michaels\|Nat]] (thus [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]) and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Enhanced\|The Enhanced]]; the latter leaked it across the NET, causing it to briefly be suspended by [[NetWatch\|NetWatch]]. [[Rache Bartmoss\|Rache Bartmoss]] took notice and used his knowledge of the NET to add a "pop-up" (unskippable) that reminds all users of what [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] is up to.
All of this has given [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] a great hand to play in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]. With [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s rep crumbling (really, who would hire out [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]]?), [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] has declared that [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]] has gone being a [[Night City/Others/Combat Zone\|Combat Zone]] far too long and has invaded it.

Keep in mind [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] has been holding on to about 30kg of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Beauties\|Beauties]] from [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Piranhas\|Piranhas]]. 

Now that [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Easy\|Easy]]'s dealt with, [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|Blood Razors]] encroach on [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] territory as they're fleeing the [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] invasion of [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]].
