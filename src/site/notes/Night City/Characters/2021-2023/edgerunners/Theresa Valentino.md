Leader of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|the SudAm cartels]] in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]].
Not much is known about [[Night City/Characters/2021-2023/edgerunners/Theresa Valentino\|Theresa Valentino]] other than that she's really proud of her heritage. [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]]'s shipments of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Coca\|Coca]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/SynthCoke\|SynthCoke]] all bear her symbol, the black crow, and all members have sworn in one way or another allegiance to [Santa Muerte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Muerte).

If you've crossed [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]], [[Night City/Characters/2021-2023/edgerunners/Theresa Valentino\|Theresa Valentino]] will *personally* make sure you pay for it, with a lot of torturous removal methods of organs and limbs. Not even [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Cyberware\|Cyberware]] can put you back together after that.

She considers herself a Saint of [the Bony Lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Muerte) (and who's to say she isn't?).
