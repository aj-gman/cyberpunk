[[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]] is the faction started by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Hex (GM)\|Hex]] and [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Ray (GM)\|Ray]] upon their arrival to [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]. 
They were soon joined by [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]], a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Street reporter]] with ties to both [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]].
After gaining some notoriety, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Liam Brightburn\|Liam Brightburn]] joined the group during [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Seven Nation Army\|Seven Nation Army]].
