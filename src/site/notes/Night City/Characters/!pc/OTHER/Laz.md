Not much is known about the reclusive [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]]-affiliated [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]]-for-hire that joined the [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Total Entropy Tour]], apart from the fact that he dresses well[^1], has the mannerisms of an [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]] and has a particular love for gruesome [[Rules/CPRED/Netrunning/Quickhacks\|Quickhacks]].

After the [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]], [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Laz (GM)\|Laz (GM)]] was rumored to have returned to [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] for one last job. After a gas-leak accident in the center of [[Eurotour/Locations/St. Petersburg\|St. Petersburg]], not much was heard of him or his whereabouts. 

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Ray (GM)\|Ray]] and [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Hex (GM)\|Hex]] have gone on to [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]].

[^1]: High-fashion, custom-tailored suits, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]]-sewn with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] licensing. Worth [[Rules/CPRED/Quality\|up to 2000eb]] a piece.
