**GM-only: [[Night City/Characters/!pc/OTHER/Ray (GM)\|Ray (GM)]]**

Ray Null? Everybody's heard of Ray Null! [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Best class smuggler]], he can get anything from point A to point B, as long as its both points are in the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]]. Forgeries, nuclear parts, tons of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/SynthCoke\|SynthCoke]], you name it, he's sold/bought/traded it! Also, he's backed up by one of the toughest [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Families]] around: [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nolasco Family/Nolasco Family\|the Nolascos]]. Only problem is, he's got a bit of a temper, that [Spanish Revolutionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Revolution_of_1936) blood in him - he's skyrocketed his fame with the stunts he pulled in [[Eurotour/Locations/Liverpool\|Liverpool]], holding an [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] in the sky near the MI6 headquarters as [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] sung his revolutions from the side doors.

>***Out-of-character:* Emy, what are three things anyone who would do a bit of digging on [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] find out about him?**
>1. Wanted by the Interpol since 2015. Mandate renewed in 2021.
>2. Sentenced to death by [[Eurotour/Organizations/MLA\|the UK]]. 
>	The British Combined Forces have an open contract on him: Dead or Alive.
>3. Is a public enemy of [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]].
>
>**What are three things you'd like [[Eurotour/The Crew\|The Crew]] and other viewers of [G-man's Cyberpunk](/) to know about [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]]?**
>1. He views himself as an anti-corpo Che Guevara: anything from edgerunner groups, to boostergangs, to crime organizations, he wants to unite them against corporations in a grand revolution. 
>2. He's a big proponent of 'work hard, play hard', but with a Eurosolo twist: after a gig he can be found enjoying a bottle of natural wine (stolen from a corpo who can afford it), reading a e-book on leftist theory, maybe romancing an output (speedware salsa, anyone?). 
>3. His favored tactic in gigs involves his internal holster, an AR and infiltrating the middle of an enemy crowd. At that point, his bad temper is the driver to his success: nobody expects autofire coming out of a tuxedo!
>
>**Sum up in a few words [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray's]] relationship with [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|The Gang]].**
>If he lives after fulfilling his dream of starting a revolution, Ray will write a book of leftist theory based on his experiences. In it, he will describe [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] as **the** exemplary archetype for the [[Night City/Organizations/NUSA\|New American]] [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]]: screwed by the system he tried building himself for, using the skills developed by his corporate society to build a new life, [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] isn't only a hired gun or a box of tools. He is creative, cooperative, and when the situation calls for it, deadly. He owes his life to his deuteragonist, and he considers him part of 'su familia'. 
>If he doesn't live, then surely [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]] will be the one to write a book about him. Initially, he didn't think much of her: just a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]] sent by [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Big Wu\|a Fixer]] to record their exploits. After a few gigs, Ray noticed that [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]] wasn't afraid to get her hands dirty, and that made him respect her. This, coupled with the fact that she is the one who records his words and actions, makes her into a prime candidate for the role of voice of his revolution.
