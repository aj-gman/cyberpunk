#npc #4cw [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]], [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realist]]
Howard Wong was the Exec who orchestrated the acquisition of NBC and the expansion of N54 programming into broadcast and entertainment. He also oversaw the creation of IMT.

In 2005, Wong was wooed away from N54 by the offer of a position as CEO of Diverse Media Systems, the LA-based mediacorp famous for introducing the braindance to the world. With his knowledge of N54’s inner workings, Wong annexed a series of key stations, industries and bureaus worldwide into DMS, turning it into N54’s only real competition.

In 2009, IMT’s Spec Ops Department, on behalf of N54, kidnapped Wong from DMS’ head office. DMS’ reaction was shooting down the aircraft escaping with Wong, killing him rather than let him fall back into N54’s hands.
