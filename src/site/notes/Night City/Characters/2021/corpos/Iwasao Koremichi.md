#npc #4cw #dead [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]], within the Banking branch

**Iwasao Koremichi** was born in the mountain city of Nagano, into a corporate family. He joined [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] when he was 29, and worked his way up until he was appointed the representative of the [Osaka](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Osaka "Osaka") office branch. Eventually, he was sent to [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] to cultivate ties with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]] following the end of [[Night City/Locations/Night City#Resurgence\|the Mob Wars]].

Iwasao is the speaker for the company often times representing the corporation in big events, while also tending to meetings with other board members or other agencies coming to [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] for their services, although he formally operates within [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s Banking division.

Iwasao Koremichi is always accompanied by his personal guard, [[Sho\|Sho]].

He is the [[Night City/Locations/Night City#Districts\|District Manager]] of the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center\|City Center]], although his personal assistant, [[Aoki Tomomi\|Aoki Tomomi]], takes care of the day-to-day of administrative work.
