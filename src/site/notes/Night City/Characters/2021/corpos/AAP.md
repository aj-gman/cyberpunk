AAP (Automated Alertness Protocol) is a service provided by the [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]], fed by a restrained network of [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Medias]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunners]]. Its purpose is to provide early-warning information regarding street-level safety for civilians across [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]'s controlled zones.
