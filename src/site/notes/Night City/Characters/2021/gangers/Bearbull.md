Archetype: Secret Weapon (Mystique, Innovation) | Nimble, Unassuming, Independent  

[[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Bearbull\|Bearbull]]'s got a few screws loose since his brain got fried during a NET Raid on his office during the [Third Corporate War](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Corporate_War). Word is he used to be a big-shot financier at [MA&F](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Merrill,_Asukaga,_%26_Finch), encouraging hostile takeovers and screwing over those who wanted to be more than just [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]].

Now, his knack for finances has garnered him a reputation amongst the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Piranhas\|Piranhas]] as *a predictor of the future*. He knows when and who will attack locations, which has saved [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Corpse Reviver\|Corpse Reviver]] at least 3 times. In exchange for his services, he is supplied a constant stream of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/SynthCoke\|SynthCoke]], which he also peddles on the street to [[Night City/!society/Strata/Movers\|Movers]] and [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] alike.
