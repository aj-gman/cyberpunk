#npc #4cw [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techie]]
[[Night City/Characters/2021/!archetypes\|!archetypes]]: **Maverick Leader** (Innovative, Power) | Pioneering, Irreverent, Entrepreneurial

*It's easier to screw over a [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] when you don't wanna screw her...*
*“Someone said [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] made her. Nah choom, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] lost her. Big difference.”*

**Raven Verrill** had it all - loving parents, a good husband, a villa out in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Rancho Coronado\|Rancho Coronado]]. She was the epitome of a [[Night City/Strata/Beavers\|Beavers]]. Soon, she'd become a [[Night City/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] - move back into the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center\|City Center]], become promoted to even higher up positions...

But corpo life wasn't for her - accident on a trip to [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Westbrook\|Westbrook]] via [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] made her realize how close she was to dying in a fraction of a second without anything to be remembered by. Her [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AV]] had crashed in the [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Badlands\|Badlands]] - and all the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Raffen Shiv\|Raffen Shiv]] saw was corporate blood. She went [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis\|cyberpsycho]] that moment, using her very few implants to repel the attackers. Her two children died in the attack.

That was three years ago. She was hospitalized and ended up in an institution in [[Night City/Locations/Dependencies/Pacifica\|Pacifica]] for "recuperation". Her husband left her, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] offered her a premium package to return to work as soon as possible (and begin working in the R&D department), but she'd had enough.

When some [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] jumped a corpo kid on the Pacifica Bridge, she massacred the boy. By 2020, she was on the mean streets of [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]] killing indiscriminately. She's got her own gang and she's been recruiting more and more [[Night City/Strata/Streetscum\|Streetscum]], outfitting them with lethal [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Cyberware\|Cyberware]] and sending them out to scavenge for [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|The Great Work]].

Unbeknownst to her, [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|The Great Work]] has been commissioned by a long term enemy...
