#npc #4cw [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]], [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelist]]
Ex-boosterganger, ex-keyboard player of Samurai, Bes Isis became an investigative journalist for N54 after 2008, becoming a major Media presence on the network due to her persistent pursuit of stories nobody else dared to cover.

In 2011, Bes documented the `Long Walk`, the exodus of 600.000 nomads from Panama and Colombia into the USA. The nomads were civilian contractors employed by the USA in the 2nd Central American War, abandoned by the government after the war’s end. After reporting the news, Bes Isis participated in the Long Walk herself. The Long Walk claimed more lives than the war itself, and gave rise to numerous Mexican Nomad gangs and coastal pirates. The long march ended with only 10% of participants, 60.000 Nomads, returning home in 2012. Bes was nominated for a Pulitzer Prize for this story.

Currently, she is likely covering the Shadow War’s more sensitive stories.
