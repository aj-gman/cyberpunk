Born out of rage, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Billy Night\|Billy Night]] resents his father's "achievements" while also still contending with his blatant murder. If he could, he'd murder and maim [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]], cause he's just as responsible as [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Richard Night\|Richard Night]] in regards to turning [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] into a hellhole.

While under [[Miriam Night\|Miriam's]] roof, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Billy Night\|Billy]] started hanging out with the "wrong crowd", including a brief affair with [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Corpse Reviver\|Corpse Reviver]] when they were both teenagers.

Now, he spends most of his time competing in the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Wild Things\|Wild Things]]' tournaments for cash and rep, under the moniker "[Faun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faun)".
