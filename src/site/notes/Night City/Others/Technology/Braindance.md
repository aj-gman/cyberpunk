The big brother of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Wireheading\|Wireheading]], [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|the BD wreath]] allows the user to record their experience (emotions, physical sensations and thoughts) via their [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Cyberware\|Interface Plugs]] and share it with others through "[[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|BD chips]]". 

This is the best [simulacrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulacrum) of real experiences money can buy and many are willing to pay any price, since it bypasses Virtual Reality to offer the consumer a near-perfect replica of the recorder's lived-in moment-to-moment experience.

The issue is, of course, [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|Braindance]] content needs original footage, even if its edited down into something digestible. You cannot "create" [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|Braindances]] out of thin air, you need someone to live through all of that.

**Braindance Inc.** is currently the sole proprietor of the [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|Braindance]] technology, although they have licensed the production of the [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|BD wreath]] to other companies, most notably [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/DMS\|DMS]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] through [a collaboration in 2007](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Braindance_Inc.).
There are other *less safe* (and less legal) [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|Braindance]] productions. [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|Pirate BDs]] are made by proficient [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techies]] to either copy or bootleg an existing [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|BD chip]] for cheaper (sometimes backed by corporate sponsors like [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]]) or to record illegal content (smut, gore, violence, usually backed up by some [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Boostergangs of Night City\|gang or another]]).

In [[2021\|2021]], at least 70% of all available [[Night City/Others/Technology/Braindance\|BDs]] contained erotic or pornographic material.
