If you're thinking of the "dark web", you're probably just thinking of a [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Boostergangs of Night City\|ganger-operated]] [[Night City/Others/Technology/IntraNET\|IntraNET]] with a ton of weird [[Rules/CPRED/Netrunning/ICE#Black ICE\|Black ICE]] that could probably kill you.

[[Night City/Others/Technology/IntraNET\|IntraNETs]] are common across strata, from [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] running E-Bulletin Boards[^1] to avoid [[Night City/Organizations/NCPD\|NCPD]] scans, to [[Night City/!society/Strata/Streetscum\|Streetscum]] using them to peddle drugs via digital silk roads and even to [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] running one to avoid the hassle of setting up a [[Night City/Others/Technology/Data Fortress\|Data Fortress]].

[[Night City/Others/Technology/IntraNET\|IntraNETs]] are cheaper, they connect multiple devices across realspace and, best of all, require little security since they can be accessed remotely only by possessing a physical dongle.
In formal speak, [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] refer to their [[Night City/Others/Technology/IntraNET\|IntraNET]] as an [EPN (Enterprise Private Network)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intranet#Enterprise_private_network), since the way they are set up makes them more akin to a [VPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_private_network) than anything else.

[^1]: See the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/Afterlife\|Afterlife]]'s infamous EBB.
