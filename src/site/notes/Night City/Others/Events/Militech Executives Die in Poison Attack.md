#news #4cw [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], **July 2021**, [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Into the Lion's Den\|Into the Lion's Den]]

This morning, seventeen [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Executives]] were found killed in their homes or personal vehicles. Correlating these 17 is the common method of *suspected* assassination: highly lethal poisons with a large delay in their activation, allowing them to be ingested without triggering standard poison detection alarms.

Whoever the culprit, they had access to advanced medical technology in order to manufacture or acquire such drugs.

[[Donald Lundee\|Donald Lundee]], CEO of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], issued a statement condemning the *"planned attack"* and went on to accuse rival company [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] for being *"behind the plot"*. This follows heated debates between the two corps as [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]] has tried to negotiate their peaceful exit from the [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/OTEC\|OTEC]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/CINO\|CINO]] hostile takeover...
