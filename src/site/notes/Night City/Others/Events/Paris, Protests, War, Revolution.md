#news #4cw **September 2021**, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]

This is [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|Lyle McClellan]], reporting on the ground in [[Eurotour/Locations/Paris\|Paris]].

For three continuous days many [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|French]] provinces have erupted in flames. Protests initially started by students in [[Eurotour/Locations/Paris\|Paris]] and homologated across the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] and even in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] (see [[Riots at the NCU campus\|Riots at the NCU campus]]) have turned violent, with [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|the French government]] bringing in their own military. We are seeing increased use of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Lazarus PMC\|Lazarus forces]], which have dispersed crowds in Marseilles with non-negligible casualties.

The reason is a general disregard of the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] and especially its most influential members, [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|France]] and [[Eurotour/Locations/Germany\|Germany]], of the living conditions of young people. Little career prospects besides becoming [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] as well as an increased focus on military spending and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]] actions abroad have made living decently impossible in the largest cities. Many have sought to move outside the metropolis, but they've been met with huge private properties leased by companies to giants like [[Biotehnica\|Biotehnica]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Petrochem\|Petrochem]], [[Continental Brands\|Continental Brands]] etc.

We cannot help but link this uptick in violence with the [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|recent concert]] of [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]] [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]] in [[Eurotour/Locations/Paris\|Paris]] and his spreading of [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|anti-corporate values]]. His extended team included known [[Eurotour/Locations/United Kingdom\|United Kingdom]] fugitive [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]]. 

What will happen next remains to be seen. Is it good, is it bad? 
This is [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|Lyle McClellan]] from [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]...
==I'll give you the questions, I'll give you the evidence, but you've got to put together the answer.==

#### Interview with a protestor[^1]
(editor's note: this interview has been transcribed from French; [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|Lyle]] was operating with a [Verlan French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verlan) [[Night City/Others/Technology/Cyberware/Language Chips\|Language Chip]])
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|N54]]:** What's it looking like from your perspective, *reuf*[^4]?
**François:** We've had to get *breliques*[^2] to protect ourselves from the *keufs*[^3]. This ain't European, none of it is, but I'd rather die on the streets of this forsaken city than labor for the people's *republique*. `(a hint of sarcasm)`
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|N54]]:** I'll stop you right there, François. What I don't get is how this is different from any other [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|French]] revolt?
**François:** Because this ain't about us, *choom* `(heavy-handed sarcasm)`. Fuck the [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|French]]. This is about a movement. A movement from [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|the corporate dream]] to our dream: a world where everyone can do just about anything. People, not organizations.
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|N54]]:** So you're a [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelist]], then?
**François:** Sure, *barjot*[^5], whatever rocks your boat. Ideology don't matter. We do.

`(François turns directly at the camera, flips it off then spits on the lens)`
