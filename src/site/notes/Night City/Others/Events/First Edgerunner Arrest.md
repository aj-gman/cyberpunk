The [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] has made the first public arrest under the new [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|emergency policy]], with known [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Rockerboy\|Rockerboy]] [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Johnny Silverhand\|Johnny Silverhand]] ending up in [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|city's finest]] [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center#Civic Center\|Civic Center]] jailhouse. Whether or not his placement next to [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|founder and coordinator]] [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]] in the floorplan is intentional, one thing is clear: this spells doom for any prospecting [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] looking to get a buck in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]].

At the same time, many smaller #corporation s are complaining about [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|the new law]] since many of their greywater jobs relied on contract workers like [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Nomads]], [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techies]] and [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunners]]. With this new law, all the gig workers must now be employees, which has gravely impacted the market's capacity to increase profit margins.
