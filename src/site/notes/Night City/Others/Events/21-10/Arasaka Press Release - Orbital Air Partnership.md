**October 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] Press Release**

We are pleased to announce that the [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/NC Municipal Airport\|NC Municipal Airport]]'s cargo and personnel services are provided in collaboration with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|our company]]. 
[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Orbital Air\|Orbital Air]] had previously signed a contract with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] for security but they've been in talks with us for ~3 months before its expiration.

Today marks the end of that relationship and thus the start of a new, fruitful labor of love.
The [[Night City/Locations/Buildings/NC Municipal Airport\|NC Municipal Airport]] will be safer than ever. We understand our role in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]'s [[Night City/Locations/Night City#Economy\|Industry and Economy]] and we take it very seriously. We will make sure that all imports and exports arrive on time, without delay, no matter the cost.

*Because [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] is the City on the Edge of Tomorrow.*
