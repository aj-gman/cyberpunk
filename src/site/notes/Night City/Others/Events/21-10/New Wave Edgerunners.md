**October 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Bes Isis\|Bes Isis]]**

[[Night City/Others/Events/Militech Executives Die in Poison Attack\|One of the Militech Executives killed in the poison attack]] is Ella Griffith. She had two children: [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Natalie]], a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Solo\|soldier]] within [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]'s ground forces in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] who also did a tour in the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]], and the deceased [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Jason Michaels]], killed in an accident just a few years ago.

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] is one of the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Techie\|Techies]] hired by the [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Entropy]] [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]]. He looks a lot like [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] by the time the first stills from [[Eurotour/Adventures/1 Paper Trail\|the Liverpool tour]] come out. In fact, one would say that his voice matches perfectly with recordings of the deceased if we compare the [[Eurotour/Adventures/4 Menace in Venice\|recordings of him]] and [[Eurotour/News/Glamorous Deaths in the Swiss Alps\|the disappeared]] [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Tammi Lu\|Tammi Lu]].

[[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] has ties to [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]. He's friends with a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Solo\|Solo]] from the [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|NC]] platoon, coincidentally a woman called [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Relevant NPCs/Nat\|Nat]]. [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] also has strong ties post-[[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]] with [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|known agitator]] [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]], who stands against all that is corporate.

Furthermore, [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] has been involved in numerous actions against [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]. The leaked [[Night City/Others/Events/Arasaka in Maelstrom\|Arasaka files]] and street reporting confirm [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]]'s involvement in destroying the [[Night City/Others/Events/The Great Work\|Punknaught]] that [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] built on [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] funds[^1], as well as his rescuing of [[Night City/Characters/2021/gangers/Easy\|Easy]], a known [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]] leader that had previous ties with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]].

We reached out to [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] for a comment. He failed to respond. 
A bad investigation would end here, forcing the viewer to assume that [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] is a [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] agent. The next conclusion is, of course, that [[Night City/Characters/!pc/The Gang\|his associates]], including [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ray Null\|Ray Null]] and [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Ze\|Ze]] - a heavily-influential member of [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]] - are also [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-affiliated.

In a world where [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Morgan Blackhand\|the greatest Solo]] works as their security advisor, it seems like everyone who has a bone to pick with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] just joins up with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]].

But [[Night City/Characters/!pc/Hex\|Hex]] is one of the founding members of a growing organization known as [[Night City/Organizations/ASG\|ASG]]. It goes right against [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|the recent Arasaka bans]] in order [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|to promote real change]]. Whether or not it is successful, its too early to tell. At least he is trying.
