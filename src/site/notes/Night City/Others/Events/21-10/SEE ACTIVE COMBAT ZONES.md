**October 2021**, [[AAP\|AAP]]
The following are **ACTIVE COMBAT ZONES**. They are not standard [[Night City/Others/Combat Zone\|Combat Zones]], meaning that emergency services are still operating in the regions, although at a reduced capacity due to emerging threats.

- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Asia#Japantown\|Little Asia#Japantown]]: Armed conflict between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Tyger Clawz\|Tyger Clawz]]
	- ==(Guerilla) Urban Warfare.== Watch out for [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AVs]], Combat Mechs and speeding vehicles.
	- ==Improvised Explosive Devices== detected.

- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Asia#Little China\|Little Asia#Little China]]: Three-way conflict between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Weng Fang Tong\|Weng Fang Tong]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]
	- ==(Guerilla) Urban Warfare.== Watch out for [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AVs]], Combat Mechs and speeding vehicles.
	- ==Improvised Explosive Devices== detected.
	- ==Secure Areas.== [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Weng Fang Tong\|Weng Fang Tong]] has declared **Emergency Shelters** for the local population. They are cooperating with the [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp#NEMA\|NEMA]].

- [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]]: Military conflict between [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]
	- ==Urban Warfare.== [[Night City/Others/Technology/AV\|AVs]], Combat Mechs, Speeding Vehicles, Snipers, Tanks.
	- ==Improvised Explosive Devices== detected. Prefer [[Night City/Others/Technology/NCART\|NCART]] or secure travelling (see [[CombatCabb\|CombatCabb]] [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|SNC]] special offers) whenever possible.
	- ==Real Possibility of Bombardment.== Install the [[AAP\|AAP]] and know where the nearest **Bomb Shelter** is in case the [[AAP\|AAP]] alarm is triggered. Emergency Shelter is provided within the perimeters established by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp#NEMA\|NEMA]] towards the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/University District\|University District]].
	- **==🔴RECOMMENDED COURSE OF ACTION: VACATE THE PREMISES.==**
