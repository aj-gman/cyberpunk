**October 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Lyle McClellan\|Lyle McClellan]]**

In the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center#Bank Block\|Bank Block]], a few tuned out cars are parked in front of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|the city]]'s greatest BBQ (real or not, for a price): the [[Sunset Bar & Grill\|Sunset Bar & Grill]]'s near-permanent imperial guard, comprised of tattooed honchos hailing directly from Hong Kong or American born'n'raised, all part of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Sun On Yee\|Sun On Yee]] [[Triads\|Triads]]. They're still licking their wounds after a battle just a few hours ago...

...in the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center#Medical Center\|Med Center]], motorcycles rev up as the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Tyger Clawz\|Tyger Clawz]] are employing street tactics to get what they want. Despite usually wearing suits and conducting entrepreneurial meetings with likeminded individuals, tonight they're assassins, killers and maimers. They're hunting for territory.

They've already had success in the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Nightlife District\|Nightlife District]] and are now encroaching on the [[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center\|City Center]]. The [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] shipments and freight that keeps falling into their hands is definitely helping.

[[Night City/Locations/Districts/City Center\|City Center]] is ablaze as the streetlights cut off. The only lumens here come from the muzzles of [[Rules/CPRED/Combat/Smart Guns\|Smart Guns]] tonight.
