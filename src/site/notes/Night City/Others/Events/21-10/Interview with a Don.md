**October 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Fiona Hayes\|Fiona Hayes]]**

**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Ahh, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Fiona Hayes\|Fiona]]! Come on, dearie, have a seat. Should I have [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|Scotty]] fetch you somethin' or another?
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** How does it feel to finally be in jail, [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|old man]]?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Surprisingly comfortable, truly. Looks like the whole [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] budget's stuck up in the prison system. I've gotta admit - I'm glad a man of the people is finally benefitting from the luxuries that only white-collar [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]] get to experience in prison.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** There are accusations that you're still running things in [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]] from inside.
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** And there are accusations that I keep [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Richard Night\|Night]] locked in a basement for my amusement.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Still, [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]] has taken quite a fall since your imprisonment - [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Romano Skiv\|your nephew]]'s struggling against the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Russians]].
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Eh, business goes up, business goes down. We've got ventures in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]] since they finally opened it up. Glad [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|NC]] is rid of those grimey [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|razors]].
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Did you hear about the recent [[Night City/Others/Events/21-10/Arasaka Press Release - Orbital Air Partnership\|Arasaka-Orbital partnership]]?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Yeah, buddy of mine told me 'bout it while I was in the sauna over yonder. This whole [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]-[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] thing is particularly bizarre, since the good people of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|NC]] are caught in the middle of the worst firefight of the century.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** You'd like the [[Night City/Organizations/States/NUSA\|NUSA]] to intervene, then?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** And take away our freedom, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Fiona Hayes\|Fiona]]? Absolutely not. I'd want [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Miriam]] to do something about it, cause she's the legacy ruler this city has. Her or [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|her company]]. Or that son of hers, [[Billy Night\|Billy]] or whatever his name is.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Looks like you've got your plate full if you get out of prison, [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Mr. Skiv]]
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Not if, but when. They have nothing on me - I shot a [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis\|cy-raging]] [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|corpo]], didn't even kill 'im.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** You did, in fact, cause the death of that man, whether you intended to or not.
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Skiv]]:** Guess it's up to [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|the coppers]] to decide, then.
