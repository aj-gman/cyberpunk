#news #4cw [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], **July 2021**, [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Deep Six\|Deep Six]]

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s stock has fallen by 10.5%, causing massive loses to the company's planned projects, including the shutdown of many construction projects in [[LEO\|LEO]] and in the eastern [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]].

While the claims are still disputed, data (emails, videos, shards) have been leaked anonymously on the [[NET\|NET]], gaining virality after the [[NET\|NET]] crashed for 29.3 minutes. When it came back, [[NetWatch\|NetWatch]] was not able to remove the pop-up appearing on every browser instance: *"Read the [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] Files"*.

The files contain supposed links between the heir to the empire, [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Kei Arasaka\|Kei Arasaka]], and the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Boostergangs of Night City\|CZ gang]] known as [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]]. While weak evidence, these have stirred quite the outrage amongst the general populace of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]].

We reached out to Lucas Yang-Zho, one of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s key negotiators in the recent economic investments into [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Night Corp\|Night Corp]]. He issued a formal apology to the shareholders affected by this sudden influx of false information and assured us that all of these claims would be disproven with time.

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s stock is still reeling after its 3.7% fall two weeks ago, when [[Donald Lundee\|Gen. Donald Lundee]] of the rival [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], accused [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] of carrying out the assassination of 17 [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|high-ranking members of the corporation]].
