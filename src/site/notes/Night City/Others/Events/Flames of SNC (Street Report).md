#news #4cw [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Toshi News\|Toshi News]], **July 2021**, [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Deep Six\|Deep Six]]

At 5:55AM, the first of the impromptu barricades on the northern "border" of [[Night City/Locations/Districts/South Night City\|South Night City]] were broken by [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] APCs. Five minutes later, the streets of the entire block were empty. 

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] support (2 squads of 35 members) came at 6:01AM and began marking using [US&R](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/national-preparedness/frameworks/urban-search-rescue) tactics. However, many of the residents were escorted out and later arrested. By 6:23AM, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] officers were beating some of [[Night City/!society/Strata/Streetscum\|the residents]] after one of them experienced a [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis Symptoms\|cyberpsychosis attack due to lack of care]].

Thanks to some help from [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Blood Razors\|the local gangs]], [[Night City/!society/Strata/Streetscum\|residents]] were able to acquire some military-grade weaponry and defended their homes from the illegal incursion. However, this success was short-lived, as [[Night City/Others/Technology/Drones\|Drones]] entered the "battlefield" and made quick work of the people.

By our estimates, the number of casualties in this forced occupation ranges in the hundreds, with many more needing expensive hospitalization. [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] has declined to comment, of course.
