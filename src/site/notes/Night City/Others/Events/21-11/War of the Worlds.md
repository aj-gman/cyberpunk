**November 2021, [[WMS\|WMS]]**

Attacks ring out across the African Ecuador as the [massdrivers](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Mass_Driver) of the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|European Space Agency]] are being assaulted in what is now confidently dubbed the [[Night City/Fourth Corporate War\|Fourth Corporate War]].

Both [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] are trying to launch their own troops into [[LEO\|LEO]] as a counter to the [[Night City/Others/Events/21-11/Global Trade at a Standstill\|latest EEC sanctions]]. Hoping to invade Tycho and Copernicus, riots are being reported from [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] insiders currently residing in [the Crystal Palace](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Crystal_Palace).

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Orbital Air\|Orbital Air]] has declined to provide either corporation with access to their massdrivers or [[LEO\|LEO]] platforms like the [Johnson Space Platform](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Johnson_Space_Platform). Worries amongst investors are growing as leaked details from [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]]'s SpecOps division shows a planned land invasion of the **Mojave [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Orbital Air\|Orbital Air]] Facility**.
