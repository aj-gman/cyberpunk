**November 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]**

[[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]], through [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]], has frozen the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|European]] assets of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] across all [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] members, from buildings to gear to liquids or stocks. [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/IEC\|IEC]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/EBM\|EBM]] are already moving to take the material assets and integrate them in their own supply chains, while [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]] is working out the details on the way any of these mergers will work under the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]]'s antitrust laws.

Many [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|European]] [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|businesspeople]] have benefited from this sudden upheaval, with rumors that the [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] had leakages that allowed insider trading propagating many so-called [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] of the ==L'Avant-garde Sanglante== (the political party [[Night City/Others/Events/Law and Entropy\|currently leading France]]) to become rich overnight.

International conspiracy, including connection with underground [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|Netrunner]] [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Enhanced\|cells]] from [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]], is suspected.
