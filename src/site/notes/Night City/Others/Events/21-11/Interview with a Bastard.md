**November 2021, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]], [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Fiona Hayes\|Fiona Hayes]]**

**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Do you hate your father?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** No. I despise his actions and his ancient way of thinking, but I do not hate him.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** But given the chance, you'd kill him?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** It'd rid the world of a great evil, but I'm afraid that each head cut down leaves room for two more to grow in its place.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** You're talking about [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Kei Arasaka\|your brother]] here?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** Among others. [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] is what my father truly believes in. Even if he's the [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|the Ideal hero]], he sees the world like a great game of chess. His only desire is to win. He sees nothing else.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** So that's why you became an [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]]? Aren't you afraid [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|of the repercussions?]]
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** I think the [[Night City/Organizations/States/NCPD\|NCPD]] has their hands full with the [[Night City/Fourth Corporate War\|the War]] at the moment.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Seems so. Isn't it a bit immature to start [your own biker gang](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Dragons) and then leave them in the dust and come to [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]?
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** If I was so immature, you wouldn't be interviewing me right now.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** Perhaps you're just a curious case of the bastard son too haunted by the empire he's supposed to rule and, because of it, your little tantrum is causing the worry of more than just your household.
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** I'm willing to die for what I believe in, unlike you.
**[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]]:** But are you willing to live for what you believe in? Dying isn't heroic, [[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Yorinobu]], it's weak.
**[[Night City/Characters/2021/edgerunners/Yorinobu Arasaka\|Y. A.]]:** I will not die until I rid the world of the great demon that resides within [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|this family]].
