**November 2021, [[WMS\|WMS]]**

Due to the increasing number of casualties from the [[Night City/Fourth Corporate War\|largest corporate conflict]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] has declared their neutrality in the ongoing war. As such, premiums have risen across the board and a strict "no-uniforms" rule has been imposed, automatically cancelling any and all calls from [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|corporate]] [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] or [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] users.

[[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] has expressed their deepest regrets to the affected users and has put out a call for international peace dealings with the help of [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Merill, Asukaga & Finch\|Merill, Asukaga & Finch]]'s top representatives. [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] has not responded, while [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] has accused the Japanese firm of "cancelling peace discussions", with [[Donald Lundee\|Gen. D. Lundee]] quoted as having said *"Those fuckers would do anything but give up!"*.

Starting **November 2021**, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] will only respond to civilian calls. The new T&C has much laxer rules on what constitutes grounds to charge the "combat rate" of the service, so users beware!
