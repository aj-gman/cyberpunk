**September 2021**, [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|French]]-[[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Soviet]] [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]]

[[Night City/Others/Events/Paris, Protests, War, Revolution\|Paris is ablaze]], fueled by [[Eurotour/Characters/Core Crew/Jack Entropy\|Jack Entropy]]'s recent [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]].
A new #boostergang has formed in [[Eurotour/Locations/Paris\|Paris]], spreading to [[Eurotour/Locations/Brussels\|Brussels]] and in other neighboring cities. They call themselves the *"Entropies"* and their sole declared purpose is to steer trouble.

The [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] is already considering a Parliament-backed bill that would outlaw [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|the practice of mercenary work, i.e. edgerunners]], in a similar vein to the recent policy change in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]], [[Night City/Others/Events/NIGHT CITY BANS EDGERUNNING\|see here]].

This has been heavily critiqued, especially by [[Eurotour/Locations/France\|France]]'s new [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]]-led political coalition, ==L'Avant-garde Sanglante==, which won in a landslide victory after a forced stepping down from power of the previous government.

Meanwhile, in [[Eurotour/Locations/Germany\|Germany]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Eurobank\|Eurobank]] continues its recommended embargo on [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] transports, with reported warfare between the two #corporation s in [the Baltic Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_Sea).
