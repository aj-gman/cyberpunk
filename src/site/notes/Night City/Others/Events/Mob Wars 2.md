#news #4cw [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/N54\|N54]] **August 2021**, [[Night City/Gigs/2021/Fated, Faithful, Fatal\|Fated, Faithful, Fatal]]

Following [[Night City/Characters/2021/corpos/Bruce Skiv\|Bruce Skiv]]'s arrest ([[Night City/Others/Events/Bruce Skiv arrested following corporate incident\|read more about it]]), high-ranking members of the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Organitskaya\|Organitskaya]] (the Russian outfit suspected of affiliations with the [[Eurotour/Organizations/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]]) have gone to raid and assault various businesses in [[Night City/Locations/Districts/Little Europe#Little Italy\|Little Italy]]. 

The [[Night City/Organizations/NCPD\|NCPD]] have deputized [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] to intervene, which has quickly turned into a bloodbath. The [[Eurotour/Organizations/EEC\|EEC]] has commented on the affair, calling it "deeply troubling".
