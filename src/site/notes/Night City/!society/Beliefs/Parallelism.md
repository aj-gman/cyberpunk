Parallelism in the [[Rules/Cyberpunk Universe\|Cyberpunk Universe]] refers to the belief that contemporary structures are inefficient but impossible to tackle head on. As such, it posits that communities must develop alternative institutions and societies.

In terms of aesthetics, it is closely related to [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Fashion/Entropism\|Entropism]].

### Principles
- The status quo does not care about the individual, it suppresses and alienates him;
- Violence against the current system will only reinforce the system;
- Those who choose to succumb to the system are at fault for it still existing;

While [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] are the most numerous believers in [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]], [[Night City/!society/Strata/Streetscum\|Streetscum]] & [[Night City/!society/Strata/Movers\|Movers]] adhere to some principles, fusing it with [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] in order to synthesize a good framework for them to live in. [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]], thus, is the most difficult to define, since it is just "living against the grain", "despite it all". Interestingly, reaching the heights of the [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]] brings with it a form of [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]] through detachment from the struggles of ordinary life.

For a [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelist]], the [dichotomy of control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoicism) separates what he cares about from what he doesn't. Easier said than done, many believers still shift their day-to-day activities enough so that they can control them - examples include becoming a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Nomad]] or an [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunner]].

People are not inherently against one another, it is the infrastructure of the system that drives them into evil and conflict. Therefore, [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]] asks to identify friend from foe and maintain that distinction of the Other. This is different from [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|Insurrectionism]], which uses the image of the Other in order to attack and deflect blame towards.

[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelists]] build a life ==outside== of the system as much as they can, living off the grid and even constructing entire societies in parallel, with their own rules and customs. They won't hesitate to steal or kill, but it won't be the first thing they think of.

### Patrons
[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]] is the most divided #ideology, since it relies on the frameworks provided by the Patrons on how to act in order to distance oneself from society at large. As such, Patrons might not agree with one another or even regard themselves as [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelists]].

### Further reading
[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]] is based on the decentralized movements of peaceful anarchism like [civil disobedience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_disobedience). The closest real-life counterpart is [Parallel Polis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Polis) through [Charter 77](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter_77). The way of living of the [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelist]] is inspired by activism ([direct action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_action) especially) and [prefigurative politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefigurative_politics).
