Realism in the [[Rules/Cyberpunk Universe\|Cyberpunk Universe]] refers to the belief that although not [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|ideal]], corporate governance is currently the best system. It posits that one must game the system in order to secure a decent lifestyle.

In terms of aesthetics, it is closely related to [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Fashion/Neomilitarism\|Neomilitarism]].
### Principles
- The corporate game was rigged from the start; the only way to win is to rig it in your favor
- Corporate success is measured by the amount of politics and subterfuge you engage in to secure your place
- Everything is a zero-sum game; if you want to be on top, you have to put others down

Most [[Night City/!society/Strata/Movers\|Movers]], [[Night City/!society/Strata/Mallplexers\|Mallplexers]] and [[Night City/!society/Strata/Beavers\|Beavers]] are firm believers in [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]]. While the thought sometimes sneaks into the minds of [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] or [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]], it isn't the dominant belief among either group. [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] keeps the lower part of the corporate ladder at war with itself, allowing very few to rise into the status of [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] (and even fewer still, if any, to [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]]).

[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|A Realist]] will always look at the world with skepticism. They will never take anything for granted - if something seems too sweet, its because **it probably is**. This means they'd rather turn down an opportunity for fear of their beliefs being reinforced. At the same time, [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realists]] might look for **easy ways** to cheat the system, like picking up side gigs, selling off valuable info etc. At some point, these risky side hustles do catch up to them.

[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] is constantly perpetuated by [[Night City/!society/Strata/Corpzoners\|Corpzoners]] (and to a lesser extent, [[Night City/!society/Strata/Powerdealers\|Powerdealers]]) indirectly by their adherence to [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealism]] and all of its precepts. Thus, those under them become disillusioned with that thought and instead revert to a disgruntled and paranoid perspective on it. [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] is the inadvertent result of [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealism]].

[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realists]] cannot comprehend [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Parallelism\|existing outside of the system]] and view [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|fighting against the entire system]] as a pointless struggle that only drains and exhausts. They believe that, by conserving their energy, fighting **from within** will go unseen and will give them a tactical advantage.

### Patrons
[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realists]] often still end up looking to [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Idealism#Patrons\|Idealist Patrons]], although they perceive them as having stacked the deck in their favor in order to reach their position. Other [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realists]] see legendary [[Night City/!society/Strata/Edgerunners\|Edgerunners]] as emblems of success. More often than not, the **Patrons** do not share the [[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realist]] ideology.

### Further reading
[[Night City/!society/Beliefs/Realism\|Realism]] is based on the general apathy of the masses in regards to the [late-stage capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_capitalism) of contemporary society. This apathy has translated into the belief that **there is no alternative** and civilization may only alter a small portion of it. This belief is called [capitalist realism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalist_Realism) and it could be argued it is [a weaponized form of cynicism masquerading as ideology](http://www.autodidactproject.org/other/cynzizek.html).
